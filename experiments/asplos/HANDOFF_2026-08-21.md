## The one-line status

The DuckDB many-to-many join is the project's first real-application victim.
On Intel it clears the quiescent capacity gate at four of five admitted sizes
(peak 2.354x) and, under a real streamer, shows a **causally attributable but
small** allocation effect: +0.058 and +0.093 in tax units at two
bandwidth-matched levels, both 95% CIs excluding zero. The largest real tax is
1.467x, against a 2x bar. **AMD is not done and is where the effect is expected
to be larger.**

## Things I got wrong this session, so they are not rediscovered

- Claimed "no Intel configuration reaches 2x". Over-claimed: a gate whose
  minimum mask exceeds the victim's reused set measures granularity, not
  capacity, and two of my three rungs came from invalid gates in opposite
  directions.
- Presented a "time-per-traffic elasticity law". It is a ratio of ratios whose
  ladder span equals its denominator's span to within rounding, and it has no
  predictive power for the tax — the lowest-elasticity host had the highest
  HNSW ratio. What survives is the invariance: capacity buys traffic, not time.
- Fixed the probe side at 10M rows, which is 5x the entire moscxl CCX of
  never-reused scan.
- Gave the streamer 3 s to settle where it needs more than 20.
- Used `pgrep -f` for liveness three times and matched my own command line
  each time. Match on `comm`, never on argv.

## Two claims for the paper that came out of the panel and are not yet in it

- **RocksDB already ships this contract in software.** `fill_cache=false` on
  the compaction read path (`compaction_job.cc:1179`,
  `block_based_table_reader.cc:1784`) reads a sealed SSTable's block, uses it,
  and never admits it — H2, by default, for a decade, with no hardware
  counterpart. Better evidence for the missing admission cell than any
  microbenchmark, and checkable at a line number.
- **The abstract must say *memory-resident* sealed SSTables.** A published
  Cassandra result has `O_DIRECT` compaction cutting victim p99 5.2x while
  *improving* compaction throughput — a deployed alternative that recovers the
  victim for free. It only works because DMA plus DDIO means the core never
  touches the lines, so it is inapplicable once the object is memory-resident.
  Unqualified, it sinks the frontier claim.
