# M12 outcome: the label works, and partitioning still beats it. But the trade-off is now measured on both axes.

Pre-registration `M12_ISOPROTECTION_PREREG_2026-08-28.md` (`cbc1e35`) plus two
amendments, each written before the data they govern. Runners and analyzers
committed before their runs. mos181, hit rate 0.5, `--reps 20`, all table sizes
exact powers of two (no cell rounded).

Three passes: **A** the tenant's cost at a mask that protects the neighbour
(n=10, 120/120); **C** an attempt to isolate stream residency by occupancy
(n=10, 80/80); **B** the neighbour itself (n=10, 60/60 --- see the section below).

## Two instrument defects, both mine, both caught and registered before amended data

1. **Amendment 1.** The occupancy sampler covered the whole 2.333 s process while
   the measured scan window is **0.180 s** --- 7.7%. A maximum over a
   variable-length window is also biased by duration, and the slower flush arm
   duly reported higher occupancy for that reason alone. 37 records quarantined
   as `m12a_VOID_duration_biased.jsonl`. Fixed with `--reps 20` (window ~77% of
   lifetime) and a post-delay **median**.
2. **Amendment 2.** P1 was unevaluable as written. `llc_occupancy` is a
   **total**, and the label moves its two components in opposite directions:
   stream residency should fall while table residency *rises*, because the table
   gets the mask to itself. At a 128 MiB table in a 128 MiB mask the second
   dominates and the total goes up. I had registered a total as a test of a
   component.

## Pass A --- the tenant's cost. The label recovers almost nothing.

Cost CoV: **median 0.28%, worst 3.70%**, well inside the 8.0% that P2's threshold
was calibrated against. This is the best-conditioned data in the campaign.

| tenant's table | mask penalty, stream retained | mask penalty, stream non-allocating | **label recovers** |
|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 32 MiB | 0.1% | −0.0% | 0.1 pp |
| 64 MiB | 1.3% | 1.2% | 0.1 pp |
| **128 MiB** (= the mask) | **16.7%** | **15.7%** | **0.9 pp** |

At table = mask --- exactly where M10 located the effect and where the label should
act --- confining the tenant costs it **16.7%**, and non-allocating the tenant's
own stream recovers **1.0 of those 16.7 points**.

The proxy's own charge is flat at **17.2--18.1%** across all six cells, masked and
unmasked, at every table size. If the label were recovering real harm, that charge
would shrink where the harm is. It does not.

- **P2 FAILS**: −3.3% against a registered >= +8%.
- **P3 HOLDS as expected**: net cost is not lower under the label.

**Registered consequence, fired:** the cost-asymmetry claim has **no silicon
instance in this benchmark**. Its cost axis must rest on gem5, which bounds it
**from above** (red-team S1-1), and the paper must say so.

## Pass C --- the occupancy instrument is unfit, so P1' is unreadable

| table | occ retain | occ flush | ratio | implied stream residency |
|--:|--:|--:|--:|---|
| 4 MiB | 18.3 MiB | 25.2 MiB | **1.379** | 14.3 -> 21.2 MiB (+48%) |
| 8 MiB | 48.0 MiB | 35.9 MiB | **0.748** | 40.0 -> 27.9 MiB (−30%) |

P1' required <= 0.60 and the 4 MiB cell reads 1.379, so P1' **fails as
registered**. **I am not firing amendment 2's consequence on it**, because the
instrument fails its own consistency check three ways:

- occupancy CoV is **33% median, 59% worst** --- larger than the effect the 0.60
  threshold was meant to detect;
- the two table sizes disagree in **direction**;
- implied stream residency varies **2.8x** across a 4 MiB change in table size,
  for a fixed 1 GiB stream in a fixed 128 MiB mask. That is not physical.

Amendment 2's registered consequence would have indicted `join_range_flushbehind`
and therefore M9, M6's flush arms and pass A. Acting on a metric that fails its
own consistency check would be the same error as opening M11b's `setup_c`
investigation on a p=0.44 result. **P1' is unreadable, not failed.**

**CMT occupancy is abandoned for this question.** The fit instrument already
existed and we had already used it: a *victim* integrates stream residency across
its whole working set and reads out at 0.01% CoV, which is how M5 established that
flush-behind removes the stream's harm completely. That is pass B.

Pass C's cost data is, like pass A's, clean and consistent: a 4 or 8 MiB table
pays **nothing** under an 8-way mask (65.297 vs 65.270; 66.573 vs 66.605), and the
proxy charge is again flat at 16.9--17.8%.

## Pass B --- the neighbour. This is the pass that goes *for* the mechanism.

n=10, 60/60. Victim CoV **0.03--0.66%** --- the best-conditioned instrument in the
project. **F's liveness asserted in all 40 co-run records** (alive at the end of
V's window *and* `HOT_TABLE_WARMED` emitted); this is the check M6b lacked and
the AMD `n=6` WC arm is why it now exists.

| arm | V cyc/load | vs `Vnone` | CoV |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| `Vnone` --- V alone, unconfined | 78.058 | 1.000x | 0.03% |
| `Vwide` --- V alone, confined to 12 ways | 88.261 | **1.131x** | 0.21% |
| F co-running, no mask, stream retained | 176.247 | 2.258x | 0.66% |
| F co-running, no mask, **stream non-allocating** | 102.337 | **1.311x** | 0.66% |
| F co-running, 8-way mask, stream retained | 87.555 | 1.122x | 0.22% |
| F co-running, 8-way mask, both | 87.459 | 1.120x | 0.19% |

### The label works, and this is the measurement that shows it

**Non-allocating F's stream removes 75% of V's harm** --- 2.258x to 1.311x --- with
**neither party confined**. That is the well-conditioned confirmation pass C could
not provide, and it independently corroborates M5. Whatever is wrong with CMT
occupancy here, the proxy demonstrably removes the stream's residency.

### `Vwide` is the control that makes pass B readable, and it fires

**P5 FAILS: V pays 13.1% for its own confinement, with nobody else on the
machine.** So P4's threshold used the wrong denominator --- I set it against
`Vnone` when the only readable comparison is against `Vwide`. Against `Vwide`:

> V co-running with F under the mask reads **0.9920x** of V's own confined
> baseline (permutation p = 0.0006). F cannot make V faster, so that 0.8% is a
> systematic --- most plausibly uncore frequency scaling with 16 more active cores.
> The correct reading is that **F's interference under the mask is at or below
> that systematic, i.e. not detectable.**

P4 "failing" at 1.122x is therefore an artifact of my denominator, and P5 is
exactly why it was registered. **This is a case where the control worked.**

Why does confinement alone cost V 13.1% when its 162 MiB working set fits in
192 MiB? Because **restricting ways cuts associativity, not just capacity**: a
random pointer chase at 84% occupancy of a 12-way cache takes conflict misses it
would not take at 20 ways. That is a general property of way-partitioning and it
is a cost the protected party pays, which we had not previously measured.

### The four options, priced on both parties

| approach | V's slowdown | F pays | V pays for the mechanism |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| do nothing | 2.258x | 0 | 0 |
| **label only** | **1.311x** | **~0** (ideal; +17.7% via our proxy) | **0** |
| **CAT, 8 ways** | **1.122x** | **+16.7%** | **+13.1%** |
| CAT + label | 1.120x | +16.7% | +13.1% |

Three things fall out, and all three are measured:

1. **They do not compose.** Adding the label under the mask buys **1%**.
2. **F strictly prefers the label** --- 0 against +16.7%, at any duty cycle.
3. **CAT delivers a better outcome for V, but not a free one.** Of CAT's final
   1.122x, essentially *all* is the mechanism's own charge rather than residual
   harm; of the label's 1.311x, all is residual harm and none is mechanism.

### One extension, with its assumption stated rather than buried

CAT's 13.1% is paid **continuously** --- a static mask charges V even when F is
idle --- while the label's 31.1% is paid **only while F streams**. Equating them
gives a break-even streamer duty cycle of **42%**: below that, the label is better
for the neighbour than a static 8-way mask.

**The assumption is that the mask outlives the streaming job**, which is how CLOS
provisioning is normally deployed but is a claim about deployment, not a
measurement. What does *not* depend on the assumption: for a **fused** tenant the
stream and the reuse interleave within a thread at morsel granularity, so no
operator can toggle the mask per streaming phase --- the mask must be held for the
tenant's whole run. That is the paper's existing "fusion defeats context scope"
argument, now with a price on the **neighbour's** side as well as the tenant's.

## What this does to the project's position

Pass A settles the axis I opened this experiment to win, and it settles it
against us: **at a mask wide enough to protect the neighbour, the tenant's own
stream is not what is costing the tenant.** The 16.7% is the tenant's table
against the tenant's mask, which is the same finding M8, M9 and M10b converged on
from three other directions. Three independent attempts to locate a
stream-attributable cost inside the fused tenant have now failed.

What that leaves is pass B, and it is a different and better claim than the one
I set out to prove. **Restated from what M12 actually measured:**

> An object-scoped label removes **75%** of a neighbour's harm while confining
> neither party. Way-partitioning removes essentially all of it, but charges the
> tenant **16.7%** and the neighbour **13.1%** --- and the neighbour's share is
> associativity loss it pays whenever the mask is in place, whether the tenant is
> streaming or not. The two do not compose: applied together they are 1% better
> than partitioning alone.

That is a **quantified trade-off on both parties' axes**, which no prior arm of
this campaign had. It is not necessity, and it does not need to be: the honest
contribution is that a page-scoped declaration buys three quarters of the
protection at zero allocation cost to anyone, and that this is the only mechanism
in the taxonomy that can do so for a tenant whose stream and reuse interleave
inside one thread.

Three claims that must **not** be made from M12: that the label helps the
tenant's own table (pass A: 0.9 of 16.7 points), that the label composes with
partitioning (1%), and that any of this transfers to AMD, whose harm is fill-path
rather than capacity.
