# M11b pre-registration: the last suspect, `setup_c` vs `setup_b`

Written 2026-08-28 **before any M11b data exists**. M11's registration committed
to this run in advance: *"if M11 fails to reproduce M6's number at M6's fact size
and reps, the helper becomes the remaining suspect and gets its own run."* It
did fail, so this runs.

## State of the gap

| | fact | reps/warm | mask helper | n | F's cost |
|---|---|---|---|--:|--:|
| M6 pass A | 256m | 4/1 | `setup_c 2 32-47 8` | **3** | **+7.5%** |
| M11 | 256m | 4/1 | `setup_b 2 32-47` | 8 | **+21.3%** |
| M11 | 1g | 1/2 | `setup_b 2 32-47` | 8 | +37.9% |

M11 localised most of the spread to fact size (15.0--21.5 pp) with a smaller
reps/warmups term (1.4--14.3 pp). What it did **not** explain is the residual
between +7.5% and +21.3% at M6's own configuration. Two candidates remain: the
resctrl helper, and M6 pass A's n=3 at a hit rate where our own replicate
analysis (M8's duplicated cell) shows ~18% run-to-run spread.

## Design

Identical to M11's `256m`, `4/1`, 256 MiB table, hit rate 1.0 cell, changing only
the helper.

- Arms: `none` (torn down), `setup_b 2 32-47`, `setup_c 2 32-47 8`.
- n=10 (higher than M11's 8, because this is a small matrix and the quantity is
  noisy at this hit rate).
- Interleaved with a per-rep rotation, schemata captured per record, aborts on
  `HOT_TABLE_ROUNDED`, A6.19, resctrl torn down on every exit path.
- No victim runs, so cpu 8's group is idle in the `setup_c` arm --- which is the
  point: if the helper matters with an idle complement, something is wrong with
  our understanding of CPU-based CLOS, and that is worth knowing on its own.

## Registered predictions

- **P1.** `setup_c` and `setup_b` agree within **5 percentage points** of F's
  cost. Rationale: both give F's CPUs a 2-way mask; `setup_c` additionally pins
  an 18-way complement to an idle CPU, which cannot draw fills.
- **P2.** Both land in **[15%, 30%]**, i.e. neither reproduces M6's +7.5%, which
  would leave M6 pass A's n=3 as the sole remaining explanation.

## Registered consequences

- **P1 and P2 hold** --- the helper is exonerated, and M6 pass A's +2.9%/+7.5%
  are attributed to **n=3 sampling at a noisy operating point plus a
  partly-resident stream**. Both are withdrawn and re-priced from M11.
- **P1 fails** --- CPU-based CLOS behaves differently under `setup_c` with an idle
  complement than under `setup_b`. That is a finding about our apparatus that
  affects **every** experiment using `setup_c`, including M6 pass B, and it gets
  its own investigation before anything is quoted.
- **P2 fails** (something reproduces +7.5%) --- whichever knob does it is the
  explanation, and M6's numbers are restored at that configuration with the
  configuration stated.

## What this cannot show

Intel EMR only, one cell, no victim. It prices F's own cost and cannot bear on
M6 pass B's measurement of V, which is a different quantity on a different
process.
