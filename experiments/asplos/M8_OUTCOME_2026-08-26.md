Pre-registration `M8_TABLEFIT_PREREG_2026-08-26.md` (`0263b73`), runner
`run_m8_tablefit.sh`, analyzer `m8_analyze.py` --- all committed before the run.
mos181, `tab:fused`'s `A3_16`/`B16` arms verbatim, only `--hot-bytes` and
`--hit-rate` moved. n=8, **224/224 records**, cells interleaved with a stride-3
per-rep rotation, `b4` schemata verified `L3:0=f` in every record, resctrl left
clean (0 groups).

Four of twenty ways holds **64 MiB** on this part (20 ways x 16 MiB = 320 MiB).

## What it means for the paper

§3's sentence --- "any mask narrow enough to constrain the stream constrains the
reused structure with it, because both arrive under one label" --- describes
**exactly the mechanism M8 measures**. This is not a refutation. It is the
discovery that the claim is **conditional**, and that we never stated the
condition:

> The conflict binds when the tenant's reused structure is larger than the mask
> its stream would need. Below that boundary a narrow mask costs the tenant
> nothing, and CAT does the job with no label required.

M6 already showed 2 of 20 ways (32 MiB) suffices to protect a neighbour. M8 shows
a reused structure of 32 MiB or less pays ~2--4% at 4 ways. **So for any tenant
whose reused working set fits in the mask its stream needs, shipped CAT is
sufficient and free.** The label's case is confined to tenants above that line.

That is a real and defensible position --- 169.6 MiB hot structures exist --- but it
is much narrower than "one thread, two access classes, one label," and the paper
currently reads as the latter. Both `tab:fused`'s caption and §3's body have been
edited to carry the boundary.

## The M6 anomaly now has a candidate explanation

M6 pass A found only +7.5% at a *narrower* 2-way mask with a 169.6 MiB table,
which is backwards against M8's +36% at a wider 4-way mask. The surviving
difference is the stream: M6 used `--fact-bytes 256m`, M8 uses `1g` --- four times
the streamed volume per rep. If the penalty above the boundary is the table
competing with the *stream* inside the mask, a 4x smaller stream should cost much
less, exactly as observed. That is a prediction, not a finding; M9 tests the same
hypothesis directly by holding volume fixed and removing only the stream's
residency.

## What this cannot show

Intel EMR only, one mask width, one fact size, no victim, one benchmark. It does
not distinguish "the table cannot fit 64 MiB" from "the table cannot fit 64 MiB
*alongside the stream*" --- the two readings have opposite consequences for
whether `tab:fused` is evidence about labels at all, and separating them is M9
(`M9_STREAMCONTROL_PREREG_2026-08-26.md`, registered before M8 finished and
running now).
