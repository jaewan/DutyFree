# W1.5 -- H2+H3 at an infinite snoop filter: the registered consistency check

Dated 2026-08-24. This resolves the one prediction W1 left open. It is the
control that decides whether `tab:h3sf`'s H3 column measures *snoop-filter
enrolment relief* or something else wearing that name.

`W1_OUTCOME_2026-08-24.md` closed the decisive cell (H2/infinite-SF = 1.0337x)
and recorded, in its own words, "H2+H3/infinite is still running; W1.2's
consistency prediction for it is registered and not yet evaluable." It is now
evaluable.

## The prediction, as registered

Registered in `W1.2_H3_ATTRIBUTION_2026-08-23.md` at 23:41 on 2026-08-23,
**before the H3/infinite runs existed**:

> H2+H3/infinite will be **the same as or slightly worse than** H2/infinite. I
> will call anything inside +/-5% "the same." If H2+H3/infinite comes in more
> than 10% *better* than H2/infinite, the `tab:h3sf` H3 attribution is
> withdrawn. If it comes in more than 10% *worse*, the no-retention traffic
> penalty is large and must be reported as a cost of H3.

## The result

Arms `sf_{qui,wb,h2,h3}_inf_s{1,2,3}`, n=3 each, launcher
`experiments/asplos/sf_inf_cells.sh` (committed), gem5 `356e7b7d0e`, analysed by
`experiments/asplos/analyze_sf_inf.py` (committed). Same geometry as W1:
victim `dutyfree/victim 2650 3000000` on cpu0, WB/`Streaming` aggressor
`dutyfree/aggressor 16.0` on cpu1 drawing from the CXL pool, 2 CPUs, L2
2 MiB/16-way, HNF 5 MiB/20-way, **infinite** snoop filter. The `h3` arm is
H2+H3, not H3 alone; H3 is not a standalone configuration.

```
qui : n=3  mean=33.881 cyc/access  sd=0.014
wb  : n=3  mean=46.380                sd=0.046
h2  : n=3  mean=35.025                sd=0.007
h3  : n=3  mean=36.233                sd=0.012

wb/inf tax = 1.3689x   h2/inf tax = 1.0337x   h3/inf tax = 1.0694x
(H2+H3)/inf / H2/inf   = 1.0345
```

**1.0345 -- 3.45% worse than H2 alone. Inside the +/-5% band, and in the
predicted direction.**

Neither escape clause fires:

- not >10% better, so **`tab:h3sf`'s H3 attribution is not withdrawn**;
- not >10% worse, so **the no-retention traffic penalty is not large** at this
  operating point. It is 3.45% and should be stated as such, not as zero.

The separation is clean relative to the noise: the h2 and h3 arms are 0.0345
apart against pooled sd of ~0.01 cyc/access, so the 3.45% is a real cost, just a
small one.

## Why this is the control that matters

With an infinite snoop filter there is **no enrolment charge to remove**. If H3
were doing something other than relieving SF enrolment -- if it were, say,
independently reducing shared-cache pressure -- it would show a benefit here.
It shows none. It costs 3.45%.

Set against the finite-SF column of `tab:h3sf`, where adding H3 to H2 moves the
tax 2.512x -> 1.061x, the pair reads as a single coherent mechanism: **H3 buys
exactly the thing an infinite SF already gives you for free, and nothing else.**
That is the account `W1.2` Correction 3 argued for on traffic grounds, now
confirmed by the direct control rather than inferred.

## Extending W1.4's decomposition with the new row

Everything from here down is **post-hoc mechanism, not a registered result.**
The prediction above concerned only the H2+H3 vs H2 ratio. The rows below use
the same pipeline as `W1.4_CHARGE_DECOMPOSITION_2026-08-24.md` and reproduce all
three of its infinite-SF rows exactly (142.4 / 194.8 / 147.1 cyc per victim L2
demand miss; 99.99% / 20.80% / 43.73% LLC demand hit rate), which is the
cross-check that the new row is computed the same way.

| arm (infinite SF) | cyc/access | sd (n=3) | victim L2 dmiss | excess misses | cyc / victim L2 dmiss | excess | LLC demand hit rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| victim alone | 33.8814 | 0.0140 | 713,948 | 0 | 142.4 | 0.0 | 99.99% |
| WB | 46.3800 | 0.0460 | 714,210 | +262 | 194.8 | 52.4 | 20.80% |
| H2 | 35.0247 | 0.0074 | 714,068 | +120 | 147.1 | **4.8** | 43.73% |
| **H2+H3** | **36.2328** | **0.0117** | **713,995** | **+47** | **152.2** | **9.9** | **39.67%** |

Per-miss excess removed against WB/infinite: H2 **90.9%**, H2+H3 **81.2%**.

Three things fall out.

**1. At an infinite SF the victim takes no extra misses in any arm.** 713,948 /
714,210 / 714,068 / 713,995 -- a spread of 0.04%. The infinite SF is doing what
it is configured to do: there is no back-invalidation into the victim's private
L2 anywhere in this campaign. The entire 1.3689x WB charge is inflation of the
*service time* of a miss stream that never changes size. This is the
infinite-SF counterpart of W1.4's finding that per-miss inflation is 62-80% of
the finite-SF charge; at an infinite SF it is 100% of it.

**2. The charge is shared-cache displacement, not raw bandwidth.** Aggregate
HNF evictions and DRAM read rate, means over n=3:

| arm | HNF demand misses | HNF evictions | DRAM read (MB) | DRAM reads / kcycle | victim tax |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| victim alone | 49 | 65 | 0.0 | 0.002 | 1.0000x |
| WB | 1,818,516 | 531,977 | 269.7 | 30.28 | 1.3689x |
| H2 | 1,047,880 | 84,877 | 170.9 | 25.42 | 1.0337x |
| H2+H3 | 1,589,806 | 67,501 | 295.7 | 42.50 | 1.0694x |

The victim alone is an LLC-resident chase: 714k L2 misses, 49 HNF misses, zero
DRAM traffic. H2+H3 pushes **more** DRAM read traffic than WB does (295.7 MB vs
269.7, and +40% per cycle) while costing the victim **a fifth** of WB's tax. So
memory bandwidth is not the mechanism. HNF eviction pressure tracks it far
better: 531,977 -> 84,877 (H2, -84%) and -> 67,501 (H2+H3, -87%). H2's job is to
stop the streamer evicting the victim out of the shared cache, and the counter
that moves is the eviction counter.

**3. H3's 3.45% is bought with fabric traffic, at the streamer's expense.**
Streamer-side (cpu1) rates, means over n=3:

| arm | streamer L2 dmiss / kcycle | streamer L1D miss rate | streamer L2 miss rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| WB | 11.37 | 95.3% | 44.0% |
| H2 | 10.93 | 82.1% | 44.5% |
| H2+H3 | 17.67 | **99.7%** | **88.3%** |

Under H2+H3 the streamer's own L1D hit rate collapses to 0.3% and its private L2
hit rate to 11.7% -- that *is* no-retention, observed directly -- and it
consequently puts **1.617x** (+61.7%) more traffic per cycle onto the shared
fabric than under H2 alone. `W1.2` Correction 3 measured +58% at a finite SF; the
same effect at an infinite SF is +61.7%. The two campaigns agree, and the
direction is the one Correction 3 identified: **the H3 confound runs against
`tab:h3sf`, not for it.** H3 wins its finite-SF result while handicapped by more
traffic, not helped by less.

## A cross-campaign observation, flagged as such

W1.4 reports H2+H3 at a *finite* SF at **151.1** cycles per victim L2 demand
miss (excess 8.7). H2+H3 at an *infinite* SF is **152.2** (excess 9.9). The two
are 0.7% apart.

If that holds, it is the sharpest statement of H3's function available: **with
H3 present, the victim's per-miss cost stops depending on whether the snoop
filter is finite or infinite.** SF capacity ceases to be a parameter, which is
precisely what "H3 prevents enrolment" predicts.

It is flagged rather than claimed because of W1.4's own caveat, which applies
verbatim here: the finite-SF arms come from the 2026-08-20 remeasure, four days
and one gem5 commit (`b2c6499` -> `356e7b7d0e`) away, and **their launcher was
never committed (F10)**. The geometry was verified equal from `config.ini`, but
a 0.7% agreement across a provenance gap of that size is not a number to lean
on. If this comparison ever becomes load-bearing, the finite arms must be re-run
under `sf_inf_cells.sh`'s committed sibling on the current commit. It is not
load-bearing today: nothing above depends on it.

## What this changes

- **Nothing in the paper's claims is withdrawn.** The registered withdrawal
  trigger did not fire.
- **One number is owed to the text**: H3's cost at an infinite SF is 3.45%, and
  wherever the paper describes H3 as free, that is wrong. Per SS5.1, it must be
  stated with its arm and operating point: *"H2+H3 costs 3.45% over H2 alone at
  an infinite snoop filter (2-core CHI, 5 MiB HNF, CXL-resident aggressor),
  where H3 has no enrolment charge to remove."*
- **The delta embargo is untouched.** Nothing here attributes the AMD CAT
  residual between H2 and H3; this is a gem5 result about a simulated SF.
- **W1 is now fully closed.** No W1 prediction remains unevaluated.

## Cross-reference added same day

The streamer-side cost of no-retention is **not first observed here**.

H3-via-ReadOnce measurably costs the *streamer's own* bandwidth (WB 4.34 / H2
