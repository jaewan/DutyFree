# M10b pre-registration: the apparatus control I mis-specified, re-specified

Written 2026-08-28, **before any M10b data exists**, after M10 completed.

## Why this exists

M10's registered apparatus control **P3** was:

> R(16 MiB) <= 1.10 under all three masks. 16 MiB is inside aggregate private L2
> and inside every mask, so nothing should move it. **On failure, P1/P2 are both
> unreadable.**

P3 **failed**: R(16 MiB) under `b2` is 1.1177 (it is 1.0061 under `b4` and
0.9996 under `b8`). The registered consequence therefore fires and **M10's
reading of P1 is void.**

The failure is not an apparatus fault. It is a defect in the prediction's own
reasoning, which I got wrong: a 16 MiB table is "inside" the `b2` mask only in
the sense that 16 < 32, but the 1 GiB stream allocates in those **same two
ways**, so the table is not immune there. 16 MiB against a 32 MiB mask is a
table/mask ratio of 0.5, and M10's own ratio table shows R ~1.02--1.12 at that
ratio --- i.e. the cell was already inside the regime it was supposed to control
for.

**I am not arguing the void away.** Yesterday I voided M7's e2e gate for a
0.005 miss on a window I had set badly, and wrote that re-reading a window after
seeing the number is not available to me. The same rule applies when the result
is convenient. M10's data stands as recorded; what it may be *read* to establish
is restored only by a control specified in advance, which is what this is.

## Design

Same binary, same fixed flags as M10 (`--fact-bytes 1g`, 16 threads on 32-47,
`--hit-rate 0.5`, `--warmups 2 --reps 1`, exact powers of two only, stderr
checked so `HOT_TABLE_ROUNDED` aborts).

- Arms: `none`, `b2`, `b4`, `b8` (as M10).
- Tables: **4194304 (4 MiB)** and **8388608 (8 MiB)** --- the corrected control
  points, at table/mask <= 0.25 even for the narrowest mask --- plus
  **134217728 (128 MiB)** re-measured as the P1 discriminator.
- n=8. 12 cells, 96 runs. Interleaved with a per-rep rotation, schemata and
  instantiated size captured per record, A6.19, resctrl torn down on exit.

## Registered predictions

- **P3' (corrected apparatus control).** R(4 MiB) and R(8 MiB) are both <= 1.10
  under **all three** masks. Rationale stated so it can be judged: 4 and 8 MiB
  are at most a quarter of the narrowest mask, so even with the stream
  co-allocating in the same ways there is room for the table.
  **On failure: M10 and M10b are both void for the mask-boundary question, and
  §3's mask-capacity sentence must be removed as unsupported rather than
  re-litigated.**
- **P1' (mask-capacity, the discriminator).** R(128 MiB) is ordered strictly
  by mask width --- R(`b2`) > R(`b4`) > R(`b8`) --- with R(`b8`, 128 MiB) < R(`b2`,
  128 MiB) by at least 0.15. A fixed 128 MiB table penalised progressively less
  as the mask widens is the signature of a mask boundary; a private-L2 boundary
  predicts no dependence on mask width at all.
- **P2' (private-L2 alternative).** R(128 MiB) is within +/-0.08 across the three
  masks.

P1' and P2' are mutually exclusive.

## Registered consequences

- **P3' holds and P1' holds** --- the mask-capacity mechanism is established.
  §3's sentence stays, cited to M10+M10b, and M10's matrix may then be reported
  as supporting detail.
- **P3' holds and P2' holds** --- §3's mechanism sentence is wrong and is replaced
  by an aggregate-private-L2 statement. M8/M9's headline (the penalty is
  capacity, not stream) is unaffected either way.
- **P3' fails** --- both runs void; §3's mask-capacity sentence is deleted, and
  §3 says only that the penalty scales with the reused structure's size, which
  M8 establishes without any mechanism attached.

## What this cannot show

does not separate L3-mask capacity from *any* effect that happens to scale with
