## Registered checks: all clean, and P1 fires

- **Instrument check PASSES**: `none`/`retain`/169.6 MiB/0.5 = 89.561, inside
  the registered [84.86, 93.79].
- **P3 (positive control) HOLDS** at both hit rates: a table that fits the mask
  pays 1.02--1.05x, in **both** stream conditions.
- **P4 HOLDS** at both hit rates: the flush proxy costs +12.9% and +13.5% in the
  unrestricted 169.6 MiB cell, reproducing M3's 14--19% band.
- **P1 (H\_capacity) HOLDS.** R with a non-allocating stream is **1.2551** and
  **1.3403**, both above the registered 1.20.
- **P2 (H\_stream) FAILS.**

The penalty is not merely unaffected by removing the stream's residency --- it is
**slightly larger without it** (1.2551 vs 1.2173; 1.3403 vs 1.2732). Sizing the
components as registered, capacity share = (R\_flush − 1)/(R\_retain − 1) =
**117%** and **125%**. The stream's share is zero within measurement, and
nominally negative.

> **`tab:fused`'s restriction penalty is a 169.6 MiB working set failing to fit a
> 64 MiB mask. The stream contributes nothing to it.**

In hindsight this is close to arithmetically forced, and that is the point: a
table that size cannot fit that mask whether a stream is present or not. Nobody
on this project checked, for months, whether the paper's strongest number needed
the stream at all.

## Registered consequence, applied

Per the pre-registration: **`tab:fused`'s 1.43x is withdrawn as evidence for
label scope.** §3's monotone way sweep does not size the label-scope conflict; it
sizes a table-versus-mask mismatch. Sec1 contribution (2) narrows again --- the
surviving claim is expressibility as supported by the taxonomy, with **no
hardware exhibit sizing it on Intel.**

The mechanism sentence stays true and is worth keeping separate from the number:
one thread, one label, so a mask narrow enough for the stream is narrow for the
table too. What M8 and M9 jointly establish is that this costs nothing below the
capacity boundary (M8: 0.999/1.007/1.041 at 4/16/32 MiB) and that above it the
cost is the boundary, not the stream (M9).

## The derived number that matters more than the headline

If the mask is not where the label earns its keep, the right question is what
non-allocation buys the fused tenant with **no mask at all**. M9's `none` column
(20 ways, full 320 MiB) answers it. The proxy flushes the same 1 GiB in both
table cells, so its absolute added work is table-independent, and the difference
between cells isolates the residency the stream would otherwise have taken from
the table:

| hit rate | proxy cost @ 32 MiB | proxy cost @ 169.6 MiB | residency benefit |
|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 0.5 | +12.631 cyc/acc | +11.584 cyc/acc | **+1.048** (+1.17%) |
| 1.0 | +5.777 cyc/acc | +6.338 cyc/acc | **−0.561** (−1.20%) |

**Roughly one cycle per access, in opposite directions at the two hit rates, on
medians whose CoV is 2--7%. The benefit is not distinguishable from zero.**

So on Intel EMR, for the fused tenant's own reused structure: the label's
measured value at full LLC is ~0, and the mask-conflict exhibit that appeared to
size it is a capacity artifact. Combined with M6 (a 2-way mask protects a
neighbour at +2.9--7.5%) and M8 (below the boundary a narrow mask is free), **the
Intel side of the paper now has no quantitative result in the label's favour.**

This is a derivation, not a direct measurement, and it rests on the proxy's cost
being table-independent. That assumption is defensible --- the flushed volume is
identical 1 GiB in both cells --- but it is an assumption, and it is doing real
work here.

## Caveats, stated because they are the only routes back

- **The proxy allows transient allocation.** `clflushopt` evicts lines that were
  allocated; a hardware type would never allocate them, so the measured residency
  benefit is a **lower bound**. M3b's flatness across a 32x flush-distance range
  ("there is little residency left to remove at 32 KiB") argues the true figure is
  close, but that is a defence, not a proof. A zero-cost type would not pay the
  12--15% proxy tax and would therefore show a positive benefit where we measure
  ~0 --- how much is exactly the quantity the δ-embargo forbids us from asserting,
  and exactly what gem5 bounds from above while being unable to model the
  congestion that dominates silicon harm
  (`GEM5_TRANSPORT_CHECK_2026-08-26.md`).
- **Intel EMR, one mask width, one fact size, one benchmark, no victim.** M6's
  neighbour result is untouched. AMD is untouched and remains the open question:
  its harm is fill-path rather than capacity (E3), so none of M8's or M9's
  capacity reasoning transfers, and the deciding narrow-mask cell there has never
  been run (`BROKER_OUTAGE_DIAGNOSIS_2026-08-26.md`, host down four days).
- **What survives untouched:** the taxonomy and the expressibility argument,
  RocksDB's demand evidence, H3's capability claim, W5.3's boundary map, and
  AMD's 9.87x fill-path residual at its one measured mask width.
