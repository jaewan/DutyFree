# W3.1 is already done, and the answer is a clean negative

Written 2026-08-23. `PLAN_B_REBUILD.md` lists W3.1 as new work: *"Try harder to
make the SF thrash on mos182 — vary victim WSS and aggressor footprint against
the SF, not the LLC. If it cannot be made to thrash, that is a publishable
negative and H3 should be cut or tightly bounded."*

**That experiment was run and is committed.** `run_sfpressure.py` and
`artifacts/probe_mos182_sfpressure.jsonl` in `benchmarks/e2e/oltp_index/`,
committed at `574f9fa`, analysed as `GPROBE_OUTCOME.md` §3.2. W3.1 should be
struck from the plan, and its stated consequence — bound H3 — followed.

## The result, recomputed independently from the raw

15/15 records. Victim fixed at ws=1024 KB (L2-resident), no CAT, 3 reps.
Thread count is the lever, per the standing rule that `-R` pacing carries a
known confound.

| threads | streaming L2 footprint | stream BW | cyc/access (3 reps) | mean | tax | victim L2 miss % |
|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | — | — | 20.42 / 20.42 / 20.42 | 20.420 | 1.000 | 0.00 |
| 8 | 16 MiB | 23.92 GB/s | 22.69 / 20.42 / 22.70 | 21.934 | 1.074 | 2.51 |
| 16 | 32 MiB | 23.73 GB/s | 20.46 / 20.42 / 20.44 | 20.441 | 1.001 | 0.04 |
| 24 | 48 MiB | 23.66 GB/s | 20.42 / 22.83 / 20.42 | 21.224 | 1.039 | 1.25 |
| 31 | **62 MiB** | 23.63 GB/s | 21.61 / 20.44 / 20.43 | 20.827 | 1.020 | 0.60 |

My recomputation reproduces GPROBE's table cell-for-cell.

**Quadrupling the streamer's aggregate private-cache footprint does not
increase the tax.** 62 MiB — essentially the whole socket's L2 capacity
streaming at once, against a victim that fits in its own 2 MiB L2 — reads
1.020×. The largest mean is at the *smallest* footprint. Bandwidth is flat at
23.6–23.9 GB/s across the ladder, so this varies SF pressure at fixed
bandwidth, which is exactly the intended lever.

The readings are **bimodal rather than noisy**: eight of twelve streamed reps
sit at 20.43 cyc / 0.02% miss (indistinguishable from quiescent) and four sit
at 22.46 / 3.26%, with the elevated reps occurring at t8, t8, t24, t31 —
random with respect to footprint. Taking the elevated state as real and
always-on bounds the tax at **1.0998×**. A discrete two-state outcome that
requires a streamer but does not scale with it is not SF capacity pressure;
GPROBE reads it as per-run physical page placement producing L2 set conflicts.

## Why it cannot be made to thrash, structurally

`GPROBE_OUTCOME.md` §4.1's geometry table is the reason, and it is not a
tuning problem:

| | SF entries | aggregate private L2 | SF : L2 coverage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| gem5 H2+H3 runs (`SETS=8192 WAYS=8`) | 65,536 | 2 × 2 MiB = 65,536 lines | **1.0×** |
| mos182 (SPR) | — | 32 × 2 MiB | multiple |

At exactly 1.0× coverage and 8-way associativity, set conflicts force
back-invalidation continuously even when total occupancy would fit. Shipping
parts provision snoop filters at a multiple of aggregate private capacity
precisely so that does not happen. So the finite-SF result is the response of
a filter provisioned at parity. It is legitimate mechanism work —
`H3_IMPL_SPEC.md:384` says the pressure arm was sized "deliberately far smaller
than the victim's read-shared footprint so SF capacity pressure and
back-invalidation are visible" — and that is not the error. The error would be
transferring the number to silicon as a prediction.

## Consequences

1. **Strike W3.1 from the plan.** It is done. What remains under W3 is W3.2
   (acquire an inclusive-LLC part — a lead decision) and W3.3 (Arm).
2. **Follow the plan's own stated consequence: bound H3.** Across everything
   measured, H3's charge is absent on both shipping hosts, and it cannot be
   induced on mos182 even at whole-socket streaming footprint. The defensible
   position is the one the W1 PASS branch already names — H3 as a **bounded
   capability claim**, not a performance claim — and this is now supported
   rather than merely prudent.
3. **New paper-edit requirement.** GPROBE §4.1: *"the 2.501× may not be
   presented as a quantity a reader should expect on a current Intel server
   part, and any text that implies otherwise needs to change."* Added to
   `W4_PAPER_EDIT_QUEUE_2026-08-23.md` as row 19.
4. **An apparatus instability worth knowing.** The matrix's ws=1024 `WB_cxl`
   arm read 20.42 in all three reps, i.e. it sampled only the clean state. No
   1.000× from this apparatus should be quoted as exact.

## What this does *not* settle

Whether *any* shipping part levies H3's charge. W3.2's inclusive-LLC Xeon
(Broadwell/Haswell-EP) back-invalidates private caches by construction and
remains the one candidate that could ground H3 in silicon. That is a lead
decision on acquiring hardware, unchanged by this document.
