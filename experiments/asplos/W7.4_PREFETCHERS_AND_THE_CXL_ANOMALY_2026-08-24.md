# W7.4 — Two facts about the W7 machine that were not on record: active prefetchers, and H2 *reducing* CXL reads

Written 2026-08-24 with 22/28 cells complete, before the A1/B0 cells (P1's) land.
Both findings come from re-reading the instantiated `config.json` and `stats.txt`
of cells already committed — no new runs, no apparatus touched.

Per F11's fifth fix, `experiments/asplos/` was listed first: `W7.3`, `W7.2`,
`W7.1` and the pre-registration all bear on this and are cited. Neither fact
below appears in any of them.

## 1. The W7 machine has four prefetchers per core, and they are busy

Read from `/tmp/w7/A1_B1_wb_s1/config.json` — the *instantiated* config, not the
flags:

| level | prefetchers instantiated |
|---|---|
| L1D | `MultiPrefetcher` = `StridePrefetcher` + `DCPTPrefetcher` |
| L2 | `MultiPrefetcher` = `StridePrefetcher` + `TaggedPrefetcher` |

They are not idle. `system.cpu0.l1d.inTransLatHist.Prefetch::samples` alone is
710,864 in one cell, and summed over both cores' L1D and L2:

| cell (seed 1) | prefetch transactions | demand L1D accesses | prefetch / demand |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A0_B0_wb | 15,438,785 | 28,231,250 | 0.547 |
| A0_B0_stream | 15,365,644 | 28,215,383 | 0.545 |
| A0_B1_wb | 8,760,749 | 46,429,777 | 0.189 |
| A0_B1_stream | 8,782,091 | 46,370,345 | 0.189 |
| A1_B1_wb | 14,561,328 | 48,959,252 | 0.297 |
| A1_B1_stream | 14,601,714 | 49,031,985 | 0.298 |

**Required disclosure under §5.1.** Every W7 figure — including every figure in
`W7.3` — was measured on a machine with L1D Stride+DCPT and L2 Stride+Tagged
prefetching enabled. No W7 number may be presented as prefetcher-free, and any
table that places a W7 row beside a row from a differently-configured campaign
owes this line.

### What it means for P2, which `W7.3` just falsified

The verdict does not change, and the direction of the correction is *against*
the kernel, not for it. `W7.3` reports the serial baseline at **1.40 lines in
flight** and reads it as what the un-batched loop sustains. It is not: at B0 the
prefetchers are running at **0.547 prefetches per demand access**, so 1.40 lines
is what the loop sustains *with aggressive prefetching already helping it*. The
un-prefetched figure would be lower.

The batching knob then *reduces* the prefetch ratio to 0.189 — it raises demand
accesses by 64% (28.2M → 46.4M) while prefetch transactions fall by 43% — which
is what you would expect when a sequential, perfectly-strided fact scan is
replaced by k=16 scattered hash-table probes. So knob B's +20.7% is a net figure:
it adds lane parallelism and simultaneously makes the access stream less
prefetchable.

This also supplies a **measured** candidate for the mechanism `W7.3` §6 could
only hypothesise. `W7.3` records that the A0 stream-smoke sustains 14.62 lines
where the fused join reaches 1.77 on the same machine, and concludes the limit is
the kernel. The prefetch ratios say *why* that is plausible: the smoke is a pure
sequential scan, exactly the pattern a StridePrefetcher serves perfectly, while
the join's demand stream is hash-scattered. **The 14.62-line ceiling is
substantially a prefetcher's achievement, not the core's.** That does not rescue
P2 — a benefit that only a prefetcher can reach is still a benefit this kernel
cannot reach — but it means the ≥6 target was calibrated against a ceiling
produced by a mechanism the fused loop cannot invoke. `W7.3` §6 should be read
with this memo; it is not withdrawn, and its numbers are unchanged.

## 2. H2 reads **fewer** CXL bytes than WB, and this is backwards

CXL passes over the 16 MiB fact array, compulsory = 4.00 (1 warmup + 3 reps):

| cell | CXL read | passes | stream traffic absorbed on-chip |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A0_B0_wb | 66.76 MB | 3.98 | 0.5% |
| A0_B0_stream | 61.53 MB | 3.67 | 8.3% |
| A0_B1_wb | 66.88 MB | 3.99 | 0.3% |
| A0_B1_stream | 61.54 MB | 3.67 | 8.3% |
| A1_B1_wb | 45.94 MB | 2.74 | **31.5%** |
| A1_B1_stream | 35.32 MB | 2.11 | **47.4%** |

Two separate problems.

### 2a. At A1 the stream is not a stream

A0 behaves as designed: the WB arm re-fetches the fact array 3.98 times out of a
compulsory 4, so it genuinely does not fit and the workload's defining
precondition — an immutable read stream that cannot be retained — holds.

**At A1 it does not hold.** A1's effective LLC is 20 MiB [F9.4], the hot table is
8 MiB and the fact array is 16 MiB, and the WB arm absorbs **31.5%** of the
stream on-chip. `W7.2` already recorded exactly this defect for the A1 *smoke*
references and excluded them on that ground; what is new here is that **the same
defect reaches the A1 morsel cells**, which are P1's and P3's, and it was not
propagated. Registered before P1's cells land: any A1 result must state that
~31% of the fact stream was LLC-resident in the WB arm, and A1 must not be
described as "the streaming regime."

### 2b. Non-allocation reduces CXL reads, which no reading of H2 predicts

At every cell the H2 arm reads *less* from CXL than the WB arm — 8.3% vs 0.5%
absorbed at A0, 47.4% vs 31.5% at A1. H2's stated semantics are that stream
lines are **not admitted** to the LLC. Non-admission should mean *more*
re-fetches, not fewer. The effect is large (5.2 MB at A0, 10.6 MB at A1) and
perfectly reproducible across seeds.

Three candidate explanations, and the state of each:

1. **Prefetching differs between the arms.** *Ruled out by measurement.* The
   prefetch/demand ratios in §1 are identical to three decimal places within each
   cell (0.547 vs 0.545, 0.189 vs 0.189, 0.297 vs 0.298).
2. **Inclusion victims.** If the LLC back-invalidates L2 copies on eviction, then
   under WB a fact line admitted to a thrashed LLC is evicted, killing a live L2
   copy and forcing a CXL re-fetch; under H2 the line never enters the LLC, so no
   back-invalidation occurs and the L2 keeps it for its reuse window. This would
   make the reduction a **real and previously unarticulated benefit of
   non-allocation**. *Untested.* It is checkable against the CHI controller's
   back-invalidation path and the L2 miss counters, and it is the hypothesis I
   would test first.
3. Some counter at `mem_ctrls1` attributing traffic other than fact reads.
   *Untested*, and considered unlikely because the guest binds the fact array to
   pool 1 explicitly (`BIND_POOL … node=2 pool=1`).

**Registered now, before P1 lands:** this anomaly is **unresolved**, and neither
of the two readings available to it may be selected after seeing P1.

* It must **not** be reported as a benefit of H2 ("non-allocation cuts stream
  traffic 8–16%") until candidate 2 is tested. That would be §6.6 fishing — the
  attractive reading, adopted because it is attractive.
* It must **not** be waved away as noise. 5.2 MB at A0 and 10.6 MB at A1, stable
  across three seeds, is not noise.
* It is a **pending question against every W7 traffic figure**, including the
  "22.6% of DRAM reads removed" in `W7.3` §8. That figure is not withdrawn — it
  is measured and correct as a measurement — but its interpretation shares this
  memo's open question about what H2 is actually doing to the hierarchy.

## 3. What this does not touch

`W7.3`'s falsification of P2 stands unchanged. P2 is a threshold on lines in
flight and on bandwidth in the H2 arm at A0/B1; both are measured directly, both
fail by more than 3×, and neither depends on the CXL-attribution question or on
the A1 sizing. §1 above makes the shortfall *harder* to explain away, not easier.

# Correction, appended 2026-08-24 ~04:55 — §1's framing was wrong, and the title is wrong

Appended rather than edited in, per A6.19. The original text above stands
verbatim, including the parts this corrects.

**The prefetchers are not undisclosed and were on record.** I checked the
`config.json` and the stats but not the config *source*, and the source says so
plainly. `DutyFree-Gem5/configs/ruby/CHI_config_8592.py:660-663`:

```
# Intel SPR-like prefetcher (all 4 default-ON):
# L1D: DCU Streamer (Stride) + DCU IP Prefetcher (DCPT)
# L2:  MLC Streamer (Stride) + MLC Spatial/Adjacent (Tagged)
# PF_DEGREE(_L1/_L2): stride prefetch depth, 4KB-page bound (tuning)
```

So this is a **deliberate, documented, and defensible modelling choice**: four
prefetchers matched one-for-one to Sapphire Rapids' DCU Streamer, DCU IP, MLC
Streamer and MLC Spatial, default-ON, `PF_DEGREE=4`, and a 4 KiB `page_bytes`
the comment calls "faithful." Modelling a real part's prefetchers is the right
call, not a defect.

**What this memo's title and §1 get wrong**, and what survives:

* *Wrong*: "were not on record", "undisclosed". They are on record, in the
  apparatus, with a comment stating the intent. The title of this memo is
  inaccurate and is left in place only because renaming a committed artifact
  hides the error.
* *Survives*: the choice was **never propagated into W7's pre-registration, its
  memos, or P2's calibration**. That is the real gap, and it is a gap in the
  analysis, not in the apparatus. The §5.1 disclosure line is still owed on
  every W7 figure.
* *Survives unchanged*: every measurement in §1 and §2, and both readings drawn
  from them. What the prefetchers do to the 1.40-line baseline, to knob B's
  0.547 → 0.189 ratio, and to the 14.62-line smoke ceiling is unaffected by
  whether the choice was deliberate.

**A knob I did not know existed, and it makes the open question cheap.** The
same block reads `PF_OFF_CORES`, `PF_DEGREE`, `PF_DEGREE_L1/L2` and `PF_PAGE`
from the environment. So a prefetcher-off arm costs one environment variable and
no code change. That directly tests the claim §1 makes on inference alone — that
the smoke's 14.62-line ceiling is substantially the prefetcher's achievement and
not the core's — and it would decompose P2's shortfall into "the kernel cannot
express MLP" versus "the kernel cannot be prefetched." **Not run, and not
proposed as part of W7**: W7's arms are fixed and the apparatus rule holds. It is
recorded as the cheapest available follow-up, for the lead to schedule or drop.

**Scope beyond W7.** The same config serves other Ruby/CHI campaigns in this
repo, and they show the same activity — e.g. `gem5/logs/
intel_8592_4cpu_dirtax_streaming/alone_75p` at 17,586,628 prefetch transactions
and `with_agg_75p` at 20,933,282. So the §5.1 disclosure is owed repo-wide on
Ruby/CHI figures, not only on W7's. It is **not** owed on the W8 full-system
boot checkpoint, which measures 0. Whether any published table needs the line is
a paper-edit question and goes to the held edit queue, not decided here.
