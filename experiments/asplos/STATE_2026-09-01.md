## Comparability defect, and what r5 actually traded

r3 table/LLC = **4 MiB / 5 MiB = 0.80**.  Silicon table = **32 / 60 = 0.53**.
r3 victim/LLC = **2650 KiB / 5 MiB = 0.518**, matching silicon's victim
(32 MiB / 60 MiB = 0.533).  r5 grew the HNF to 7680KiB to fix table/LLC
and **broke victim/LLC to 0.345**.  The victim is less vulnerable; that is
why total tax fell 10.74 → 6.29 cyc and why P2 landed at a marginal
1.185× against a floor that had been relaxed from r3's 1.30 to 1.15
because the LLC is 1.5× larger.

r5 is not "r3 with one knob fixed."  It swapped a matched ratio for an
unmatched one, and the ratio it broke is the one a protection metric
lives on.  The autopsy (both pieces of the tax scaled ~40%) is the
description; the cause is the shrunken victim/LLC.  Matching both ratios
at this HNF would need a ~4 MiB victim, not 2650 KiB.  That run does not
exist.  Do not start it to chase H2 R; disclose the limitation.

The join quantizes `--hot-bytes` to a power of two times 16 B: **2 MiB or
4 MiB**.  2 MiB is L2-resident and makes CAT inert.  Matching silicon
table/LLC still means keep 4 MiB and grow the HNF — but then grow the
victim with it, which r5 did not do.

> **LLC-residency threat refuted.** Working set is only 1.6× the LLC, but the

> demand across the CXL controller: the LLC supplied *none* of the measured

**Replacement:**

> **The LLC-residency threat is real and unresolved.** The 1.007×/1.009×
> agreement is a composition coincidence, not a residency result: decomposed
> by request type at the home node, `pfoff_8c`'s 2,114,960 CXL read lines are
> **1,215,867 `ReadUnique_PoC` write-allocate fetches from the setup phase**
> plus **899,092 `ReadShared` read fetches**, and only the second group can
> have served the two read passes, which need 2,097,152 lines. The
> controller-served share of the read passes is bounded above at **42.9%
> (`pfoff`), 43.6% (`h2`) and 77.7% (`wb`)** at 8 cores, so the cache
> supplies at least ~57%, ~56% and ~22%. The mechanism is the opposite of the
> LRU-reuse argument: `fill_fact` **writes** the whole working set
> immediately before the passes read it, the dirty lines land in the LLC on
> eviction from the private L2s, and because the HNF is `alloc_on_read* =
> false` and the STREAMING bypass suppresses 90–96% of the read stream's
> clean fills, the passes never displace them. The LLC ends these runs
> **94–100% full of dirty fact lines**. **The WB/H2 asymmetry therefore does
> exist**, in H2's favour: WB's clean-eviction fills (1,495,749 against H2's
> 78,955, a factor of 19) evict the resident dirty lines that H2's bypass
> preserves, which is why WB pulls **1.78× more CXL read lines to deliver the
> same bytes**. H2's higher home-node hit fraction **is** stream traffic, so
> "from non-stream traffic only" is wrong. Part of H2's measured advantage is
> consequently an LLC-hit advantage.

**What this forces on the paper.** **20–43 GB/s must not be described as CXL,
far-memory, interconnect or link bandwidth.** A large and *arm-dependent*
fraction never crossed the controller. It is aggregate application read
throughput. `Sec7_Evaluation.tex` is corrected accordingly (see below).

**What it does not change.** The ordering `H2 ≥ WB > pfoff` holds on
`agg_bw_sum`, on the union-span rate and on the fully-disjoint floor, at both
core counts, capped and uncapped. The +25% (4c) and +39% (8c) margins stand as
**floors** — a windowed denominator widens them to +34% and +45%. `agg_bw_sum`
is high by **2.4–16.7%**, not by the "at least 1.329×" the CXL-cap campaign
inferred; that inference assumed every measured byte crossed the capped
controller and is withdrawn. Every published window-overlap floor is
**confirmed**, including the 90.7% cell the cap campaign called refuted, which
reconstructs to 90.84%. `WB/pfoff` is a **ceiling**. **No re-run is required
to publish the ratios.**

**Frame it as a mechanism gain, not a retraction — with the bracket stated.**
H2 declines clean fills, which protects resident dirty data and yields more
LLC hits per useful byte. That is a real property of non-allocation and it is
the policy under test operating as designed. But it is **not** what this
experiment was built to isolate, and the write-then-read structure of
`fill_fact` is an artifact of the harness rather than a property of a
deployed workload. Do not carry the LLC-hit component as a STREAMING benefit
until a geometry that does not manufacture the residency measures it.
