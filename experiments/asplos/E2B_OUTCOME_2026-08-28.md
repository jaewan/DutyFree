# E2B outcome: the decomposition on one stream size. All four predictions hold.

Pre-registration `E2B_FOOTPRINT_PREREG_2026-08-28.md` (`1265574`), runner and
analyzer committed before the run. mos181, victim `pointer_chase` 170 MB on cpu8,
tenant 16 threads on cpus 32-47 streaming **1 GiB** from CXL at hit rate 0.5, **no
mask at any point** (runner refuses to start if any resctrl group exists).
n=8, **88/88 records**, tenant liveness asserted in **80/80** co-run records,
instantiated table size verified against the request in every cell.

Instrument check **PASSES**: victim alone 78.050 cyc/load, CoV 0.04%, inside the
registered [75.70, 80.38].

## The sweep

| tenant's own table | victim, stream retained | victim, stream non-allocating | **harm removed** | CoV r/f |
|--:|--:|--:|--:|:--|
| 4 MiB | 1.995x | **0.994x** | **100.7%** | 2.83 / 0.15 |

**All four registered predictions hold** --- the first time in this campaign that
everything registered came out as predicted:

- **P1** monotone decline with $F(4) \ge 90\%$ and $F(256) \le 50\%$: 100.7 ->
  100.7 -> 96.6 -> 74.1 -> 14.3%. **HOLDS.**
- **P2** the residue grows with the tenant's footprint, $H_f(256) \ge 1.8$:
  0.994 -> 0.993 -> 1.040 -> 1.324 -> **2.392**. **HOLDS.**
- **P3** arm identity against the one row already on this stream size: 74.1% vs
  M12's 75.3%, $|d| = 1.2$ points against a tolerance of 8. **HOLDS.**
- **P4** the strong form, $H_f(4\,\text{MiB}) \le 1.10$: **0.994**. **HOLDS.**

## What P4 holding means

P4 was the branch I registered as the one that mattered most. Had a 1 GiB stream
left the victim above 1.10x with only a 4 MiB tenant footprint, there would be a
harm component that is **neither** stream residency **nor** the tenant's own data,
scaling with stream volume --- partly reviving the M3b transport claim that M5
retired, and turning the two-component account into a three-component one.

It did not fire. At 4 and 16 MiB the victim returns to **0.994x and 0.993x** ---
below its own baseline, by the same ~0.6% uncore-frequency systematic seen
throughout. **The two-component account survives its most dangerous test**, and
survives it against a 4x larger stream than the one it was built on.

## The supersession, variable by variable

I registered that E2B would supersede rather than confirm M5 and M3b, because
their stream was partly LLC-resident. What actually happened is more specific, and
the honest version is not "stream size matters":

| row | removed | stream | hit rate |
|---|--:|---|---|
| M5, 4 MiB | 100.5% | 256 MiB | 1.0 |
| **E2B, 4 MiB** | **100.7%** | 1 GiB | 0.5 |
| M12, 128 MiB | 75.3% | 1 GiB | 0.5 |
| **E2B, 128 MiB** | **74.1%** | 1 GiB | 0.5 |
| M3b, 256 MiB | 28.0% | 256 MiB | 1.0 |
| **E2B, 256 MiB** | **14.3%** | 1 GiB | 0.5 |

- **The small-footprint end is robust.** M5's 100.5% reproduces at 100.7% across
  *both* a 4x stream change and a hit-rate change. That row was never fragile.
- **The matched-conditions row reproduces.** 75.3% -> 74.1%.
- **The large-footprint end does not.** 28.0% -> 14.3%, roughly halved. **But two
  variables differ between M3b and E2B --- stream size *and* hit rate --- so the
  gap cannot be attributed to stream size alone**, and I am not going to attribute
  it. What can be said is that at the paper's own operating point (hit rate 0.5,
  1 GiB, one-pass) the figure is **14.3%**, and that is the number the paper
  should carry.

Separating those two variables would need the same sweep at hit rate 1.0. Not
registered, not run, and worth doing only if the paper leans on the 256 MiB row ---
which it should not, since the interesting content of this table is the
*monotonic decline*, not any single cell.

## Consequences, applied

- **The dagger comes out of the paper.** The decomposition table becomes a
  five-point sweep at one stream size, one hit rate, one victim, and the
  `$^{\dagger}$`-footnoted stream-size mismatch disappears with it.
- **M5's and M3b's rows are superseded** for the paper's operating point; the
  quantity index is updated to point at E2B and to record that M3b's row differed
  in two variables, not one.
- **The two-component account finally has the exhibit it never had**: a single
  monotone sweep from complete recovery to almost none, driven by exactly the
  quantity the account says drives it.
- Quantity-index **gap 5 is closed**.

## What this cannot show

Intel EMR only, one victim type and working set, one hit rate, one stream size ---
that last being the point, but it means E2B cannot itself measure the stream-size
dependence it was created to remove. The label is still the flush-behind proxy, so
what is measured is non-allocation carrying a 13--19% charge to the tenant rather
than the memory type; that charge falls on the tenant and not the victim, so it
does not enter the numbers above. And the 256 MiB row's disagreement with M3b is
recorded as unattributed rather than explained.
