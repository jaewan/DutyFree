# W3.4 -- H3's performance case, assembled from what is already measured

Dated 2026-08-24. Written for **lead decision 4** ("Does H3 survive?"), which
`PLAN_B_REBUILD.md` flags but does not equip with numbers. It equips it. **It
does not make it** -- H3's fate is a contract-shape decision and belongs to the
lead.

Nothing here is a new experiment. Every number is from a committed campaign;
one row is computed from W1's stats for the first time and is marked as such.

## Where H3's benefit is supposed to live

H3 removes the **snoop-filter enrolment** charge. It follows that H3 can only
pay where an SF is small enough, relative to aggregate private-cache capacity,
to thrash. That is not a hedge -- W1.5 turned it into a measurement.

| SF | WB | H2 | H2+H3 | what H3 is worth |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **finite** (65,536 entries) | 2.501x | 2.512x | **1.061x** | the paper's H3 result |
| **infinite** | 1.3689x | 1.0337x | **1.0694x** | **−3.45%: a cost** |

Finite row from `tab:h3sf` (2026-08-20 remeasure); infinite row from W1,
n=3, `W1.5_H3_INFINITE_SF_OUTCOME_2026-08-24.md`. Both 2-core CHI, L2
2 MiB/16-way, HNF 5 MiB/20-way, CXL-resident aggressor.

The infinite-SF column is the non-thrashing limit. H3's benefit there is not
small; it is **absent, and negative**.

## Whether any reachable machine is in the paying regime

**W3.1, closed negative** (`W3.1_CLOSED_2026-08-23.md`). On mos182 (SPR,
non-inclusive + SF), driving 62 MiB of streaming private footprint -- the whole
socket's L2 -- against an L2-resident victim leaves the tax at **1.020x**,
bounded at **1.0998x** taking the bimodal high state as always-on, with
bandwidth flat at 23.6-23.9 GB/s across the ladder. The tax does not scale with
footprint; the largest mean is at the *smallest* footprint. Bergamo (exclusive
victim L3) is negative for a structural reason of its own.

The structural cause is `GPROBE_OUTCOME.md` S4.1: gem5's SF sits at **1.0x**
coverage of aggregate private L2; shipping parts provision a multiple. This is
not a tuning gap that a better experiment closes.

So: the regime where H3 pays is one we have not been able to reach on silicon,
and the regime silicon occupies is the one where H3 costs.

## What H3 costs the streamer, with its arm named

This is the row computed here for the first time, from the W1 infinite-SF
stats. All n=3, same runs as the table above.

| infinite SF | WB | H2 | **H2+H3** | H2+H3 vs H2 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| streamer instruction throughput (IPC) | 0.1626 | 0.1794 | **0.1196** | **−33.3%** |
| streamer committed instructions | 22,621,044 | 18,854,794 | **13,000,788** | −31.0% |
| CXL read bandwidth (GB/s) | 3.2949 | 3.0891 | **5.1685** | **+67.3%** |
| CXL read bytes per committed streamer instruction | 10.66 | 9.05 | **22.73** | **2.51x** |
| streamer L1D hit rate | 4.7% | 17.9% | **0.3%** | -- |
| streamer private-L2 hit rate | 56.0% | 55.5% | **11.7%** | -- |

Read the first and third rows together. **Under H2+H3 the declared stream does
a third less work while pulling two thirds more bytes per second off CXL.** It
moves 2.51x the CXL traffic per instruction retired. That is no-retention
paying for its soundness argument in refetches, measured rather than asserted.

The WB and H2 columns reproduce `W2.1_DECONFOUND_2026-08-24.md`'s CXL row
(3.2929 / 3.0872 there; 3.2949 / 3.0891 here, 0.06% apart -- W2.1 quotes the
`::total` figure and this quotes `::ruby.snf.cntrl`), which is the cross-check
that the H2+H3 column is computed the same way.

## A metric-labelling problem this exposes

`H3_MODELCHECK.md` S(a) already records a streamer-side cost for H3-via-ReadOnce:
"WB 4.34 / H2 5.12 / **H3 1.22 GB/s** -- no L1/L2 retention means no prefetch
MLP." That caveat is honest and it predates this memo. But **it names neither
its arm nor its operating point**, and it is not comparable to the table above:
H2 reads 5.12 GB/s there and 3.09 GB/s here.

Worse, the raw-bandwidth number moves in the *opposite direction* between the
two sites -- 5.12 -> 1.22 (down 76%) there, 3.09 -> 5.17 (up 67%) here. Both
can be true of different quantities at different operating points, and neither
is wrong, but a reader handed both would conclude one of them is. The
resolution is the throughput row: on *useful work* the two agree, because a
streamer that must refetch every line can move more bytes and still finish
less. **Bandwidth alone is the wrong headline metric for an arm that destroys
its own reuse** -- exactly the S5.1 failure the arm-identity rule exists to
prevent, found in one of our own memos.

Filed as an S5.1 instance. It does not change any published number; it changes
how H3's cost must be quoted.

## The three options, stated without choosing

1. **Keep H3 as variant (a), disclose its price.** Requires edit-queue rows 6
   and 34 and the streamer table above. The paper then claims H3 as a
   *capability* -- per W3.1's own stated consequence -- and prices it honestly:
   3.45% victim-side and a third of the streamer's throughput at an infinite
   SF, against 2.512x -> 1.061x at a finite one.
2. **Cut H3.** The paper gets shorter and every H3 disclosure obligation
   disappears. Cost: the contract loses a clause, and `tab:h3sf`'s finite-SF
   result -- the one place the mechanism shows a large number -- goes with it.
3. **Replace variant (a) with (b) or (c).** This is the option the cost table
   argues for and it is not currently in the plan as a decision, only as a
   build. `H3_MODELCHECK.md` proves **(b) epoch-tagged retention with bulk
   clear** and **(c) DeNovo self-invalidation** sound by TLC. Both retain lines,
   so both keep the streamer's MLP, so **the entire cost measured above is a
   property of the realization we happened to build, not of H3**. The kernel
   side already exists: `mm/streaming.c` has `streaming_drain_range()` and
   `streaming_writeback_all()`.

Option 3 dissolves the objection rather than disclosing it, and it is the only
one of the three that makes the paper's H3 stronger. It is also the only one
that costs simulator work. That trade is the lead's to price.

## What this does not say

- It does not say H3 is unsound, or that the soundness argument is affected.
  `H3_MODELCHECK.md`'s taxonomy stands; variant (a) is sound, vacuously.
- It does not touch the S3 delta embargo. Nothing here attributes the AMD CAT
  residual between H2 and H3; this is gem5 and mos182 only.
- It does not withdraw `tab:h3sf`. W1.5's control **confirms** the H3
  attribution there rather than undermining it: H3 delivers nothing when there
  is no enrolment to relieve, which is what "H3 relieves enrolment" predicts.
- It does not close W3. W3.2 (inclusive-LLC Broadwell/Haswell) remains the one
  experiment that could move H3 from capability to silicon-grounded benefit,
  and it remains a lead decision about hardware.
