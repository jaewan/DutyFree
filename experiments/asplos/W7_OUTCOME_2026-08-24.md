# W7 outcome — the 2×2's designed corner was never reached, on either axis

Campaign complete: **28/28 cells**, 2026-08-24 01:04–04:46 (3 h 42 m wall,
**66.2 host-hours**, 2.37 h/cell mean, ~18× parallel on mos181).
Analyzer: `experiments/asplos/w7_analyze.py`, committed before the data landed.
Pre-registration: `W7_PREREGISTRATION_2026-08-23.md` §3 plus its four addenda
(the "what I have seen" table, R1–R4, R5).

This memo adjudicates **P1** and **P5**, which became evaluable when the last
cell (`A1_B0_wb_s3`) landed, and states the campaign-level reading. **P2 was
already adjudicated** in `W7.3_P2_FALSIFIED_2026-08-24.md`, written and
committed while 6 cells were still running so it could not be shaped by them;
nothing here revises it.

## 3. P1 — hit-rate half NOT EVALUABLE; DRAM half a null at the realized point

P1 as registered (§3): *"O2 isolated (A1/B0). LLC hit rate rises by ≥15 points
WB→H2, and DRAM read traffic falls by ≥50% of the achievable saving. Falsified
below 25.8% of achievable."*

### 3a. Hit-rate half: **NOT EVALUABLE**

R5's registered condition, quoted from the addendum committed in `4790138`
**blind to both A1/B0 arms**:

> if A1/B0's WB LLC hit rate is ≥85%, P1's hit-rate half is reported as **NOT
> EVALUABLE**, not as falsified.

Measured: `A1_B0_wb` LLC hit rate = **97.685%** (per-seed 97.706 / 97.676 /
97.673, sd 0.015). 97.685 ≥ 85, so **the condition fires**. There are 2.315
points of headroom to a perfect cache and P1 asks for 15. No policy, and no
operating point of this machine, could have produced it. The measured movement
is +0.14 points — but that number must not be reported as "P1 missed 15 by
14.9 points," because the metric's ceiling was 2.3.

This is stronger than `W7.2`'s protection and distinct from it: `W7.2` says a
falsified P1 at A1 is a null at a *different* point; R5 says the threshold was
outside the metric's *range*.

### 3b. DRAM half: falsified at threshold, **but protected — the prediction stays open**

Arithmetic spelled out at the line that computes it (F11 fix #3):

```
A1_B0_wb     DRAM read   16.731 MB   (16.726 / 16.738 / 16.730)
A1_B0_stream DRAM read   15.552 MB   (15.551 / 15.553 / 15.551)
floor (one compulsory load of the 8 MiB hot table)   8.389 MB
achievable = 16.731 - 8.389                        = 8.343 MB
realised   = 16.731 - 15.552                       = 1.180 MB = 14.1%
```

14.1% < 25.8%, so the registered falsification threshold is crossed. Unlike the
hit-rate half, **this metric has range**: reaching ≥50% needed DRAM read to fall
to 12.560 MB, and the floor is 8.389 MB, so the target was reachable in
principle. The half is therefore genuinely evaluable and genuinely missed.

`W7.2`'s reading rule, registered before the campaign and printed by the
analyzer's own banner, nevertheless governs the report:

> a FALSIFIED P1 or P3 here may **not** be reported as falsifying the registered
> prediction — it is a null at a different point, and the prediction stays open.

P1 was designed for 32 MiB / 64× L2 / hot set at 25% of LLC. It ran at 20 MiB /
40× / 40%. **Verdict: a null at 20 MiB / 40× / 40%. P1 as registered is not
falsified and remains open.**

## 4. P3 — a null at the realized point, and doubly confounded

P3: *"convergence (A1/B1). cyc/access improves ≥5% WB→H2; falsified below 2%."*
Measured **+0.63%** (per-seed +0.648 / +0.620 / +0.616, sd 0.014). Below 2.

Same `W7.2` protection applies verbatim: **a null at 20 MiB / 40× / 40%, not a
falsification of P3 as registered.** Two independent confounds sit on top of it,
both registered before the relevant data:

* **R1 (B axis under-strength).** P2 is falsified: B1 delivered 1.77 lines in
  flight against a required ≥6, ~26% of the movement needed, and 12% of the
  machine's own measured 14.62-line ceiling. A null on a knob that barely moved
  is uninformative about that knob's target.
* **R5's "why A1 did this" (A axis over-relieved).** A1 was chosen to relieve O2
  so H2 would have room to work. It over-relieved it: 8 MiB of hot table in a
  20 MiB effective LLC is 97.6% resident, leaving almost nothing for an
  admission policy to protect. `W7.4` §2a adds that at A1 the *fact stream* is
  31.5% LLC-resident under WB, versus 0.5% at A0 — **A1 is not the streaming
  regime.**

## 5. P5 — dominance test fails, as disclosed in advance; sufficiency clause dead

| | A0/B0 | A0/B1 | A1/B0 | A1/B1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| H2 effect on cyc/access | +0.53% | **+1.23%** | −1.00% | +0.63% |

**Dominance: FAILS.** A1/B1 (+0.63%) is not the largest H2 effect; A0/B1
(+1.23%) is, and it is ~2× larger. This outcome was **disclosed as already
determined** in the addendum committed at `de4a28b`, when three of the four
cells were visible and 0.628 < 1.234 made the condition false for every possible
value of the unseen A1/B0 gap. It is recorded here as confirmed, not as news.
**P5's rules are not blind and this memo does not present them as such.**

**Sufficiency: dead, per R3.** §3's escape clause — "if either single knob
already delivers ≥5%, the 2×2 is unnecessary" — does not fire. Every H2 effect
in W7 is ≤1.23%. The correct statement is **"no knob tested suffices,"** not "a
single knob suffices." R4 records that `w7_analyze.py`'s else-branch previously
printed the latter (F12 in my own analyzer); wording fixed 2026-08-24, the
computation byte-identical, auditable by diff.

**P4 is not evaluated.** It needs a resctrl/CAT arm, which SE mode cannot run.

## 6. New and not predicted: at A1/B0, H2 **costs** time

This is the only cell in W7 where H2 is slower than WB, and it is P1's cell.

* **−1.00%** on cyc/access, and **all three seeds are negative** (−0.902,
  −1.634, −0.464). Arm sds are 0.271 (WB) and 0.215 (H2) cycles against a mean
  difference of 1.00 cycle, so the sign is not seed noise.
* Ruby's own load-latency means move with it: `RequestType.LD.latency_hist_seqr`
  **48.987 → 50.060 (+2.19%)**, hit component +2.48%, miss component +1.95%
  (n=3, sds ≤ 0.21).
* And it happens **while H2 reads less from everywhere**: DRAM 16.73 → 15.55 MB,
  CXL 45.84 → 35.06 MB, LLC hit rate 97.69 → 97.83%.

**The mechanism is unresolved and this memo does not assert one.** Two things
constrain any candidate:

1. Fewer bytes read from both pools and a *higher* hit rate, yet more time, is
   the `W7.4` §2 CXL anomaly showing up in the time domain rather than only in
   the traffic counters. `W7.4` leaves inclusion victims as the leading untested
   candidate; nothing here tests it.
2. The latency means are **per-request means over a changed request mix**, so a
   rise does not by itself mean any path got slower. A1/B1 proves the point in
   the opposite direction: its aggregate LD latency *rises* 0.65% while its
   cyc/access *improves* 0.63%, with hit latency falling 1.33% and miss latency
   rising 0.75%. Three of four cells have latency and time agreeing in sign;
   A1/B1 does not, which is itself evidence that this statistic is a mix, not a
   mechanism.

What it is safe to say: **H2 is not free, and at one measured operating point
its cost exceeds its benefit.** That belongs in the paper's honest accounting
whatever happens to the rest of W7.

## 7. What W7 establishes, and what it does not

**Does not establish:** that H2 fails to deliver time benefit when its two known
obstacles are removed. §4's strong-negative story required "P3 fails at A1/B1
with P1 and P2 both confirmed." P2 is falsified and P1 is a protected null, so
**that story is unavailable and §4 may not be quoted as though it were live**
(already registered in the R1–R4 addendum).

**Does establish**, and this is the campaign's actual result:

> The 2×2 was built to reach one corner — MLP relieved *and* hierarchy relieved —
> and **that corner does not exist on this machine.** The B axis reached 1.77 of
> the ≥6 lines it needed (29%). The A axis overshot: instead of giving the
> admission policy room, it made the victim 97.7% LLC-resident, leaving 2.3 of
> the 15 points P1 asks for. Every cell W7 measured is a cell in which at least
> one of the two obstacles is still fully in place.

Three secondary results are solid and independent of the falsifiers:

* **Batching works and is not the bottleneck being blamed.** −20.7% cyc/access
  under H2 and −20.1% under WB at A0, +26% bandwidth, traffic unchanged. What
  fails is the *magnitude* it can reach, not the mechanism (`W7.3` §4).
* **The kernel, not the memory system, is the MLP limit.** The A0 stream-smoke
  reaches 14.62 lines at 3.89 genuine CXL passes, so ≥6 was 41% of a measured
  ceiling — conservative, not optimistic (`W7.3` §6).
* **H2 reads fewer CXL bytes than WB at every single cell**, unexplained
  (`W7.4` §2), and now shown to coexist with a time *penalty* at A1/B0 (§6).

## 8. What would be needed to reach the designed corner

Recorded, not proposed — sizing and spend are lead decisions.

* **B axis to ≥6 lines.** `W7.3` §6 locates the limit in the probe kernel, not
  the memory system. Reaching 6 lines needs a probe that can express ≥6
  independent misses, which `--probe-batch 16` demonstrably does not; the
  measured ceiling says the machine would allow it.
* **A axis at a hot-set ratio that leaves something to protect.** The requested
  25% was the right target; F9.4 turned it into 40% *and* the absolute LLC into
  20 MiB, which made the victim resident. Any re-run must pick sizes that are
  exact powers of two after `(size/assoc)/block`, or the same defect recurs
  silently.
* Note both are needed **together**. Fixing either alone reproduces a cell W7
  already has.

Two diagnostics remain recorded-but-unrun, both lead decisions: a `PF_OFF_CORES`
prefetcher-off arm to split P2's shortfall into "cannot express MLP" vs "cannot
be prefetched," and a test of the inclusion-victim hypothesis for the CXL
anomaly.
