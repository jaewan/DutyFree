## Known gaps, stated rather than hidden

- **Broker-side runners live only on `moscxl`** under `~/DutyFree/amd_cat/`. They
  are not in this repo. That is the F10 failure mode (an unpinned apparatus) and
  should be fixed by committing them; the data files they produced *are* tracked
  under `experiments/asplos/data/`.
- **Several analyses were inline** (written into a shell heredoc at analysis
  time) rather than committed as a file. Their thresholds are recorded in the
  pre-registration, but the exact code is not recoverable. Future experiments
  should commit an `analyze_*.py` before the data lands, as the HNFRP and H2H
  ones did.
- **gem5 run outputs live in `/tmp`** and do not survive a reboot. Only the
  derived numbers in the outcome documents are durable. Where a result is
  load-bearing, its per-seed values are transcribed into the outcome document
  itself for exactly this reason.
