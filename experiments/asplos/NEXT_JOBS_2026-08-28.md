## Job 1 --- finish verifying way-partitioning (in progress)

Implementation is 210 lines across 6 files, builds clean, methods callable,
guards fire, and the per-way audit stat works. **Two checks remain and one
obstacle was just found:**

- The verification workload was too small. A 512 KB victim never reaches the LLC
  --- the HNF logged **52 accesses and zero allocations**, because 512 KB fits the
  2 MiB L2. Re-running at 4 MiB.
- **Check 1 (null test)** --- a no-mask run must be identical to the pre-change
  binary. Requires stash, rebuild, re-run, diff. Not yet done, and it is the
  check that decides whether the additive design really is additive.
- **Check 2 (mask enforcement)** --- with a CLOS confined to ways 0--7, ways 8--19
  must read exactly zero allocations. Readable off `stats.txt` once a workload
  actually allocates in the LLC.

No performance claim from the model until both pass.

## Job 2 --- a workload in the regime where admission acts

The existing gem5 fused kernel runs at **0.52 GB/s, 8x below the model's own
bandwidth ceiling**, MLP-limited by its dependent probe chain, so an admission fix
has nothing to relieve. This is the honest reason the e2e exists. It needs:

- a stream that pressures the LLC at rate,
- a reused structure held by the same thread,
- a co-resident victim (already available --- `dutyfree/victim`, used by W1).

Only then can gem5 produce the comparison the project has never made: label
versus way-partitioning, both parties priced, one environment.

## Job 3 --- the writing block

neighbour's own confinement cost, the occupancy curve, and the fact that a mask
fully protects at every split. Also missing: the masked arm's bimodality (a

boundary must become the frame, not a caveat** --- a paper claiming the trade-off
without naming the boundary will be refuted by the first reader who tests the
other regime.

Build the argument on the **tenant** side, which travels; demote the neighbour's
own confinement cost to a conditional bonus with its platform dependence stated.
