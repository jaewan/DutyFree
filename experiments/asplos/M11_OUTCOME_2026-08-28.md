**My registered branch logic was mis-ordered.** With P2 and P4 both failing it
selected "the discrepancy is in warm-up state, not footprint," which the data do
not say: fact size contributes 15.0--21.5 pp and reps/warmups 1.4--14.3 pp, so
both matter and fact size is the larger and more consistent term. The correct
registered branch is the fallback --- *"mixed; report both terms and quote no
single price for CAT."* The action on M6's numbers is identical in every branch,
so nothing turns on the mis-ordering, but the analyzer said something the data
does not support and that is recorded rather than quietly fixed.

## The finding that matters

**M6 pass A ran n=3.** Eight cells, three reps each. And M8's accidentally
duplicated cell showed that at hit rate 1.0 --- M6 pass A's operating point --- our
R estimates reproduce only to **17.5%** at n=8. An n=3 estimate at that operating
point is not a measurement we should have quoted, and it does not survive n=8.

Three causes, in order of size:
1. **Fact size.** M6's 256 MiB fact array is not a one-pass stream in a 320 MiB
   LLC; a true 1 GiB stream raises F's cost by 15--21 pp.
2. **n=3 at a noisy operating point.** Even at M6's own configuration the number
   moves from +7.5% to +21.3%.
3. **Warm-up state.** Up to 14.3 pp.

## Consequence for the paper --- this changes the rewritten claim

Contribution (2), rewritten on 2026-08-26, says we *"measure its price to the
streaming tenant at 2.9--7.5\%."* **That price is wrong by a factor of four to
five.** Re-priced from M11's one-pass arms with a 256 MiB reused table, a narrow
mask costs the tenant **+30% to +38%**.

The head-to-head from `M6_OUTCOME` has to be restated, and it is no longer
domination:

| reused table | CAT-narrow | the label (flush proxy) |
|---|---|---|
| **4 MiB** | V 0.9924 at F **+2.9%** | V 0.9928 at F +18.7% |
| **256 MiB** | V 0.9899 at F **+30--38%** | V 2.2776 at F ~0% |

- At a **small** reused structure CAT still **dominates on both axes** --- and M10b
  explains why: a table that fits inside the mask pays essentially nothing
  (1.00--1.04x).
- At a **large** reused structure it is a **genuine trade-off**, not a defeat:
  CAT protects the neighbour and charges the tenant a third of its throughput;
  the label charges the tenant nothing and does not protect the neighbour.

So "**the label has no winning cell**" --- my headline on 08-26 --- is too strong.
The correct statement is that **neither mechanism wins both axes at a large
reused structure**, and that the two are complementary in exactly the way the
panel's R1 branch originally anticipated ("division of labour, not sole rescue").
I overshot R1's verdict on the strength of an n=3 arm.

M6 pass B is **unaffected**. It measures V's slowdown, n=10, order-rotated, with
an instrument check that passed; nothing here touches it. What changes is only
the *price* attached to CAT on F's side.

the last untested knob and is registered as M11b.
