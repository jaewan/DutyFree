# M11b outcome: inconclusive, and the reason is the finding

Pre-registration `M11B_PREREG_2026-08-28.md`, runner committed before the run.
n=10, 30/30 records, M6 pass A's exact cell (256 MiB table, `--fact-bytes 256m`,
`--reps 4 --warmups 1`, hit rate 1.0), changing only the resctrl helper.

## Result

| arm | n | cyc/access | CoV | F's cost |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| `none` | 10 | 44.472 | 7.9% | --- |
| `setup_b 2 32-47` | 10 | 62.967 | 11.7% | **+41.6%** |
| `setup_c 2 32-47 8` | 10 | 56.011 | 11.7% | **+25.9%** |

Schemata confirm both helpers give F's cores the same two ways (`L3:0=3`);
`setup_c` additionally pins an 18-way complement (`L3:0=ffffc`) to the idle
cpu 8.

## Both registered predictions "fail" --- and the failure is not readable

- **P1** required the two helpers to agree within 5 pp. They differ by 15.6 pp.
- **P2** required both in [15%, 30%]. `setup_b` is at 41.6%.

The registered consequence for a P1 failure was to open an investigation into
`setup_c`, on the grounds that it would affect every experiment using it,
**M6 pass B included**. I am not opening it, because the difference is not
established:

- permutation test on the medians: **p = 0.443**
- bootstrap 95% CI on the difference: **[-13.7, +13.9]** cyc/access, spanning zero

**My 5 pp threshold was set below the instrument's resolution.** At n=10 with
CoV ~12%, this measurement cannot resolve anything finer than about +/-14 pp.
Declaring an apparatus defect on a p=0.44 result --- and then re-auditing M6 pass
B on the strength of it --- would be exactly the error this review exists to
catch.

## The real finding: this operating point cannot support the numbers we quoted

The same configuration, measured twice at n=8 and n=10 a few minutes apart:

| cell | M11 | M11b | apart |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| `none`, 256m, 4/1, hit rate 1.0 | 51.850 | 44.472 | **16.6%** |

That independently reproduces M8's duplicated-cell figure of 17.5%. So:

> **At hit rate 1.0, F's cost in this benchmark has roughly +/-15% run-to-run
> resolution at n <= 10.** M6 pass A's +7.5% (n=3), M11's +21.3% (n=8) and
> M11b's +41.6%/+25.9% (n=10) are not four measurements of four things. They are
> a badly-conditioned quantity measured four times.

Four of this week's registered thresholds --- M7's P2 (missed by 0.005), M10's
control, M11's P2 (missed by 0.016 pp) and M11b's P1 --- were set finer than the
instrument resolves. **That pattern is mine, and it is the process finding of this
review.** A threshold has to be justified against measured reproducibility, not
chosen as a round number near the expected effect.

## Where the number should come from instead

Hit rate 0.5 --- `tab:fused`'s own operating point --- is a well-conditioned
regime, and we have it. The overlapping 128 MiB cells between M10 (n=6) and M10b
(n=8) reproduce to **1.3%, 0.3% and 4.4%** across the three masks, against 16.6%
at hit rate 1.0.

**The defensible price of confining the tenant, hit rate 0.5:**

| reused table | 32 MiB mask | 64 MiB mask | 128 MiB mask |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| 32 MiB | +21% | **+2%** | **0%** |
| 128 MiB | +51% | +39% | +17% |
| 256 MiB | +41% | +37% | +28% |

Read against M10b's mechanism: the price is near zero when the reused structure
fits inside the mask, and 28--51% when it does not.

## Consequence

- **M6 pass A's +2.9%/+7.5% are withdrawn.** n=3, at an operating point with
  +/-15% resolution, on a stream that is not one-pass.
- **M11's +30--38% is also not quoted**, for the same operating-point reason.
- **Contribution (2) is re-priced from the hit-rate-0.5 measurements above**,
  which are reproducible to a few percent: **~0--2% when the tenant's reused
  structure fits the mask it is given, and ~37--41% when it does not.**
- The head-to-head conclusion from `M11_OUTCOME` is unchanged in substance and
  now rests on better numbers: CAT dominates at a small reused structure; at a
  large one neither mechanism wins both axes.
- **M6 pass B is untouched.** It measures V at n=10 with a passing instrument
  check, and no evidence here impugns `setup_c`.

## What this cannot show

Intel EMR only, one cell, no victim, and it does not exonerate `setup_c` either
--- it fails to detect a difference at a resolution of +/-14 pp, which is not the
same as showing there is none. If a `setup_c` effect below that size exists, this
run would not see it. Testing that properly needs hit rate 0.5 and larger n, and
is not run.
