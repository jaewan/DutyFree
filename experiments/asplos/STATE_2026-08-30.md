## The claim, and what supports it

**No shipped mechanism gives H1 (prefetching) and H2 (no shared-cache fill)
together at acceptable cost.** Every alternative is measured:

| mechanism | H1 | H2 | measured cost | source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| WB (default) | yes | **no** | this is the problem | --- |
| WC | **no** | yes | **3.9x** bandwidth (12.43 -> 3.20 GB/s single core) | kmod frozen figures |
| `prefetchnta` (Zen 4c) | yes | **no** | **does not work** — victim pinned at 100.00% L2 miss | `AMD_NARROWMASK_OUTCOME` |
| `movntdqa` on WB | yes | **no** | architecturally an ordinary load; null control | same |
| CAT / way mask | yes | agent-scoped only | **16.7%** silicon, **15.8–24.2%** model | `Sec5`, `FUSED_INDEX_ARTIFACT_CORRECTION` |
| flush-behind | yes | approx | **14–17%** streamer + app changes + unenforceable | `M5_OUTCOME` |
| **STREAMING (H1+H2)** | yes | yes | **~0 — tenant gains 4–12%** | `H2H_FUSED_OUTCOME` + correction |

**Why believe the model on the one mechanism silicon cannot run:** it reproduces
the mechanism silicon *can* run. The model predicts CAT's tenant cost at
15.8–24.2% against silicon's 16.7%, and its H2 recovery (88.5%) is *below* the
silicon proxy's (100.5%). It gets the measurable case right and under-claims the
unmeasurable one. Combined with its 39%-low WB tax, **every magnitude it supplies
is a lower bound.**

## H3 — DECIDED: a capability claim, not a benefit

H3 removes a snoop-filter enrolment charge. Three independent lines say no
reachable machine levies it:

1. **W3.1, closed negative.** Whole-socket scale on SPR — 62 MiB of streaming
   private footprint against an L2-resident victim — leaves the tax at
   **1.020x**, and *the tax does not scale with footprint*.
2. **The modeled regime is unrepresentative.** gem5's SF sits at **1.0x**
   coverage of aggregate private L2; shipping parts provision a multiple
   (`GPROBE_OUTCOME` S4.1). Not a tuning gap.
3. **The last candidate was withdrawn today.** Bergamo's L2-hit collapse is L3
   eviction, not probe-filter activity (`AMD_L3OCC_OUTCOME`).

Meanwhile H3 **costs**: −26.4% tenant IPC, +162.6% tenant L2 misses/instruction,
+4.53% to the neighbour, in the regime real machines occupy.

**Do not present H3 as a demonstrated benefit.** Keep it as: only a declaration
can elide enrolment, no predictor can infer immutability, and whether a part
levies the charge is a provisioning choice — at 1x SF coverage it is 2.5x and H3
removes 95.5%; at shipping provisioning we measure no charge and H3 costs 4.5%.

## The one open gap that matters

**The declaration path is never connected to the hardware results.**
`pseudo_inst::setstreaming` marks pages in the SE-mode `EmulationPageTable`
(`W8.1`), so **every gem5 STREAMING number comes from a simulator
pseudo-instruction, not from `mprotect(PROT_STREAMING)` on a booted kernel.**

- FS-mode **arm 1 (`wb`) passed its gate** — Linux boots, the workload runs.
- FS-mode **arm 2 (`stream_mprot`) livelocked** after 6h44m, and `W8.8` showed
  that livelock reproduces **bit-identically on stock CHI** — an upstream gem5
  bug, not our delta.

Closing this is worth more than any further measurement. It is triage, not
research.

## Correction 2 — 2026-09-04, the 124 µs above is a clock artifact, not a measurement

"Correction — 2026-09-04" above, added earlier today in `e9534a6`, prices
baseline epoch entry at **124 µs** from the gem5 full-system lifecycle logs.
**Its direction is right and is not retreated from here** — entry is
microseconds, the ~48 ms survives only as the default-off H3 seal oracle, and
the correction was if anything *understated*. What is wrong is the **status of
the figure**: 124 µs is not a measurement. It is one tick of the gem5 guest's
clock divided by eight. Per `A6.19` the block above is left verbatim and its
superseded wording is quoted rather than deleted:

> **Replacement:**
>
> > | the OS half | `PROT_STREAMING` in Linux 6.8; 47 kselftest / 10 KUnit in-guest; baseline epoch entry ~124 µs, no drain; ~48 ms survives only as the default-off H3 seal oracle | `W6.1`, `W8.2`, `KERNEL_TEST_AGGREGATE_OUTCOME_2026-09-03` |

**Replacement, superseding that one:**

> | the OS half | `PROT_STREAMING` in Linux 6.8; 47 kselftest / 10 KUnit in-guest; baseline epoch entry **72–90 µs on QEMU/KVM** (four boots, `data/kernel/`), no drain; the gem5 full-system figure is resolution-limited (below the guest clock's ~1 ms tick), not 124 µs; ~48 ms survives only as the default-off H3 seal oracle, on a third platform and not comparable to either | `W6.1`, `W8.2`, `KERNEL_TEST_AGGREGATE_OUTCOME_2026-09-03` |

**Why.** The gem5 guest marks its TSC unstable and falls back to
`clocksource: refined-jiffies`
(`gem5/logs/fs_boot_ckpt/atomic_2cpu_os_validation_h2_r1_16g/system.pc.com_1.device:107,190`).
At `CONFIG_HZ=1000` one such tick is **999 848 ns** — a build constant,
re-derived here from `kernel/time/jiffies.c:78-104` with
`CLOCK_TICK_RATE = PIT_TICK_RATE = 1193182`. The selftest prints
integer-truncated microseconds over `GENERATIONS 8`, so `enter_max` = one tick
= 999 µs, `enter_avg` = 999 848 / 8 / 1000 = **124 µs**, and `os_validation`'s
`exit_avg` = two ticks / 8 = **249 µs**. `enter_max=999 us` is byte-identical
across all four lifecycle logs, which a genuine maximum over independent runs
would not be: seven of eight samples returned a **0 ns delta**. The figure
bounds entry at ≲ 1 ms and says nothing finer. The QEMU/KVM family runs on
`kvm-clock` at nanosecond resolution and behaves like real data (means 90, 90,
72, 87 µs; maxima 85/156/156/168 µs).

**Three platforms, not two.** Neither family measures a `WBNOINVD` broadcast,
so **neither is comparable to the ~48 ms**, which came from a third platform —
the 64-logical-CPU silicon host (`SUBMISSION_READINESS_2026-08-19.md` C10).
The oracle build issues a machine-wide clean and the baseline issues none, so
the ~48 ms → µs change is a **mechanism** change (`888060f6a66e`,
`eccecc49e0ff`), not a platform difference.

**Source.** `KERNEL_TEST_AGGREGATE_OUTCOME_2026-09-03.md`, addendum 2026-09-04.
The same correction is carried against the current state document in
`STATE_2026-09-01.md` addendum 10, which argues there why this is an appended
addendum rather than an edit to the block above. No measurement was launched;
this is arithmetic over already-committed logs and kernel source.
