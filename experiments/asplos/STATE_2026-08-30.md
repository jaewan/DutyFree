# Project state — 2026-08-30 (end of day). Read this first.

## The claim, and what supports it

**No shipped mechanism gives H1 (prefetching) and H2 (no shared-cache fill)
together at acceptable cost.** Every alternative is measured:

| mechanism | H1 | H2 | measured cost | source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| WB (default) | yes | **no** | this is the problem | --- |
| WC | **no** | yes | **3.9x** bandwidth (12.43 -> 3.20 GB/s single core) | kmod frozen figures |
| `prefetchnta` (Zen 4c) | yes | **no** | **does not work** — victim pinned at 100.00% L2 miss | `AMD_NARROWMASK_OUTCOME` |
| `movntdqa` on WB | yes | **no** | architecturally an ordinary load; null control | same |
| CAT / way mask | yes | agent-scoped only | **16.7%** silicon, **15.8–24.2%** model | `Sec5`, `FUSED_INDEX_ARTIFACT_CORRECTION` |
| flush-behind | yes | approx | **14–17%** streamer + app changes + unenforceable | `M5_OUTCOME` |
| **STREAMING (H1+H2)** | yes | yes | **~0 — tenant gains 4–12%** | `H2H_FUSED_OUTCOME` + correction |

**Why believe the model on the one mechanism silicon cannot run:** it reproduces
the mechanism silicon *can* run. The model predicts CAT's tenant cost at
15.8–24.2% against silicon's 16.7%, and its H2 recovery (88.5%) is *below* the
silicon proxy's (100.5%). It gets the measurable case right and under-claims the
unmeasurable one. Combined with its 39%-low WB tax, **every magnitude it supplies
is a lower bound.**

## H3 — DECIDED: a capability claim, not a benefit

H3 removes a snoop-filter enrolment charge. Three independent lines say no
reachable machine levies it:

1. **W3.1, closed negative.** Whole-socket scale on SPR — 62 MiB of streaming
   private footprint against an L2-resident victim — leaves the tax at
   **1.020x**, and *the tax does not scale with footprint*.
2. **The modeled regime is unrepresentative.** gem5's SF sits at **1.0x**
   coverage of aggregate private L2; shipping parts provision a multiple
   (`GPROBE_OUTCOME` S4.1). Not a tuning gap.
3. **The last candidate was withdrawn today.** Bergamo's L2-hit collapse is L3
   eviction, not probe-filter activity (`AMD_L3OCC_OUTCOME`).

Meanwhile H3 **costs**: −26.4% tenant IPC, +162.6% tenant L2 misses/instruction,
+4.53% to the neighbour, in the regime real machines occupy.

**Do not present H3 as a demonstrated benefit.** Keep it as: only a declaration
can elide enrolment, no predictor can infer immutability, and whether a part
levies the charge is a provisioning choice — at 1x SF coverage it is 2.5x and H3
removes 95.5%; at shipping provisioning we measure no charge and H3 costs 4.5%.

## The one open gap that matters

**The declaration path is never connected to the hardware results.**
`pseudo_inst::setstreaming` marks pages in the SE-mode `EmulationPageTable`
(`W8.1`), so **every gem5 STREAMING number comes from a simulator
pseudo-instruction, not from `mprotect(PROT_STREAMING)` on a booted kernel.**

- FS-mode **arm 1 (`wb`) passed its gate** — Linux boots, the workload runs.
- FS-mode **arm 2 (`stream_mprot`) livelocked** after 6h44m, and `W8.8` showed
  that livelock reproduces **bit-identically on stock CHI** — an upstream gem5
  bug, not our delta.

Closing this is worth more than any further measurement. It is triage, not
research.

## Do NOT do

- **H3 end-to-end** — it needs the unrepresentative 1x-SF configuration and costs
  26% tenant IPC. It would add an attack surface next to a sound argument.
- **Application benchmarks in FS mode** — days per configuration for realism, not
  mechanism; the silicon side already runs real applications.
- **More gem5 sweeps** — the wedge has a 7-point curve and both boundaries.

## Health warning

Between 2026-08-28 and 08-30 the following were found and corrected: a 2x LLC
replacement bias, a power-of-two probe stride that inflated the headline wedge
2.3x, a power-of-two table rounding, **three broken verification checkers** (one
false-passing, one false-alarming, one written but never executed), eight
mis-specified thresholds, an RMID artifact that would have produced a dramatic
and wrong conclusion about CAT, and a paper that silently dropped four tables
whenever text was added.

None were caught by review. All were caught by **pre-registration, controls, and
reading values back from artifacts**. The recurring root cause is one sentence:
**the instrument did not measure the quantity in the claim.** The conventions in
the top-level `README.md` are load-bearing, not stylistic.
