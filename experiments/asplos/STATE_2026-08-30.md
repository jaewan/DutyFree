# Project state, 2026-08-30 — read this first

## Where the argument stands

The contract is **implemented on both sides** and the central claim is measured
in one apparatus.

| question | answer | where |

## What is open

1. **§1/§5 rewrite — partially done.** §5 has the same-model head-to-head; §1 has
   the AMD restatement and the scoped wedge. Not yet done: leading with ratios
   throughout and demoting absolute magnitudes, which is the recommendation from
   the 2026-08-30 assessment (every headline magnitude has moved at least once;
   the ratios have not).
2. **H3's standing is UNKNOWN.** Not "cut", not "supported". It costs the tenant
   26% IPC and the neighbour 4.53%, and no reachable machine has been *shown* to
   levy the charge it removes — but Bergamo may, and the instrument to decide has
   only just been added (`AMD_L3OCC_PREREG`).
3. **The AMD non-allocation arm is BLOCKED.** The WC path needs `/dev/cxl_wc`;
   the CXL window is online System RAM, 37 of 129 blocks hold unmovable pages,
   and offlining does not remove a block from the iomem resource tree — so the
   module's disjointness guard can never be satisfied. Freeing it needs a
   boot-time `memmap=`/soft-reserve change on a shared host.
4. **`tab:h3sf`'s "1.000× on Bergamo" does not reproduce** — the L2-resident
   victim is harmed a stable ~1.9×. Since that claim is what makes H3 a
   *capability* claim, it is load-bearing.
5. **Paper length**: 24 pages against an 11–13pp body norm.
6. **The paper's absolute AMD magnitudes are not reproducible** (27.6× vs the
   published 19.9×/20.6×) while the *fraction removed* is (55.6% vs 55%). Per
   S6.6 this should be stated.

## What I would do next, in order

1. **Consolidate, do not measure.** The repo knows materially more than the paper
   says, and that gap is where F11-class errors live. Finish the ratio-led
   rewrite.
2. **Land the `llc_occupancy` result** (`AMD_L3OCC_PREREG`) — it decides whether
   the 4 MB AMD hit-collapse is L3 eviction or back-invalidation, and therefore
   whether H3 has a claim on reachable hardware.
3. **Decide H3** on that evidence, not before.
4. Only then consider new experiments.

## Health warning for whoever picks this up

Between 2026-08-28 and 08-30 the following were each found and corrected: a 2×
LLC replacement bias, a power-of-two probe stride, a power-of-two table rounding,
two broken verification checkers (one false-passing, one false-alarming), eight
mis-specified thresholds, and a paper that silently dropped four tables whenever
text was added.

None of these were caught by review. All were caught by **pre-registration,
controls, and reading values back from artifacts**. Keep doing that; the
conventions in the top-level `README.md` are not stylistic.
