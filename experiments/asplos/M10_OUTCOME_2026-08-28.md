# M10 + M10b outcome: the boundary *is* the mask. §3's mechanism sentence survives.

Two runs. `M10_MASKBOUNDARY_PREREG_2026-08-27.md` (`8dd1eca`) swept mask width
against table size; its apparatus control failed on a prediction I had
mis-specified, which voided its reading by the registered rule.
`M10B_PREREG_2026-08-28.md` (`63877fa`) re-specified the control **in advance**
and added a sharper discriminator. Both runners committed before their runs, both
abort on `HOT_TABLE_ROUNDED`, both record the instantiated table size and the L3
schemata in every record.

## Why these ran

M8/M9 concluded the fused restriction penalty is a capacity effect, and on
2026-08-26 I wrote the mechanism into §3: *"a four-way mask holds 64 MiB and the
instantiated table is 256 MiB, so the restricted arm moves the table from a mask
it fits into one it cannot."*

**M8 did not establish that.** It varied table size at a single mask width, and
its control cells (4/16/32 MiB) are equally explained by a boundary it never
varied: 16 cores x 2 MiB = **32 MiB of aggregate private L2**. A table that size
can live in private L2 and never depend on L3 allocation at all. That is standing
rule S5.2 --- check the hot set against the private L2 before believing a null ---
applied to my own conclusion.

## M10 (n=6, hit rate 0.5, instrument check passed at 90.215 in [86.03, 95.09])

R = median(masked)/median(`none`):

| table | b2 (32 MiB mask) | b4 (64 MiB) | b8 (128 MiB) |
|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 16 MiB | 1.1177 | 1.0061 | 0.9996 |
| 32 MiB | 1.2128 | 1.0230 | 0.9966 |
| 64 MiB | 1.3531 | 1.1747 | 1.0153 |
| 128 MiB | 1.5037 | 1.3910 | 1.1592 |
| 256 MiB | 1.4098 | 1.3677 | 1.2847 |

The knee (first R > 1.15) lands at **32 MiB for b2, 64 MiB for b4, 128 MiB for
b8** --- exactly each mask's capacity. And R is close to a function of
table/mask: at ratio 1.0 it is 1.21/1.17/1.16 across the three masks, at ratio
2.0 it is 1.35/1.39/1.28.

**But M10's registered control P3 failed** --- R(16 MiB) under `b2` is 1.1177
against a registered <= 1.10 --- and its registered consequence was that P1/P2
become unreadable. So M10's reading is **void**, and the table above is recorded,
not claimed.

The failure was a defect in my prediction's reasoning, not in the apparatus: a
16 MiB table is "inside" a 32 MiB mask only if you forget that the 1 GiB stream
allocates in those same two ways. At table/mask 0.5 the cell was already inside
the regime it was meant to control for.

I did not argue the void away. On 2026-08-26 I voided M7's e2e gate for a 0.005
miss on a badly-set window and wrote that re-reading a window after seeing the
number is not available to me. That applies equally when the result is
convenient.

## M10b (n=8, control re-specified in advance)

| table | b2 | b4 | b8 |
|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| **4 MiB** | 1.0122 | 0.9993 | 1.0006 |
| **8 MiB** | 1.0380 | 1.0005 | 0.9983 |
| 128 MiB | **1.5312** | **1.3978** | **1.1163** |

- **P3' (corrected control) HOLDS.** All six control cells are within 1.038 ---
  4 and 8 MiB tables are untouched by every mask, so the apparatus is clean.
- **P1' (mask-capacity) HOLDS.** At a **fixed** 128 MiB table the penalty is
  strictly ordered by mask width, 1.5312 > 1.3978 > 1.1163, a spread of 0.415
  against the registered >= 0.15.
- **P2' (private-L2) FAILS.** It predicted a spread <= 0.08; the spread is 5x
  that.

A fixed table penalised progressively less as the mask widens cannot be a
private-L2 effect --- private L2 does not change when the L3 mask does.

> **The boundary is the mask.** §3's mechanism sentence is supported, and is now
> cited to a controlled experiment rather than to M8's single-width sweep.

## Consequence, as registered

Per M10b's registered consequence for "P3' holds and P1' holds": the
mask-capacity mechanism is established, §3's sentence stands as written, and
M10's matrix may be reported as supporting detail --- which is how it appears
above, with its void stated.

This is the one red-team check this week that came back **in the paper's favour**,
and it is worth being precise about what it does and does not buy. It confirms the
*mechanism* behind the penalty. It does not restore the penalty as evidence for
label scope --- M9 settled that the stream contributes nothing to it, and M10b's
own numbers are consistent with M9: the penalty is about the reused structure
against the mask, whatever the stream is doing.

## What this cannot show

Intel EMR only, one benchmark, one fact size (1 GiB), one hit rate (0.5), no
victim. It cannot distinguish L3-mask capacity from *any* mechanism that scales
with mask width. Hit rate 0.5 was chosen in advance because M8's accidental
duplicate cell showed R reproducing to 0.4% there against 17.5% at hit rate 1.0;
the mask-width ordering has not been re-checked at hit rate 1.0.
