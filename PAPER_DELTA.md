## 2. NOT yet in the draft — four findings, all from 2026-08-30

### D1. A way mask **restores** the victim's L3 residency (`AMD_CATOCC_OUTCOME`)

The strongest AMD result and it supersedes the §1 sentence currently there.
Occupancy under a mask, victim 4096 KB:

| arm | slowdown | victim L3 occupancy | % of quiescent |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| `wb` | 27.6x | 180 KB | 5.5% |
| `cat8` | 12.4x | 2820 KB | 85.7% |
| `cat1` (**one way**) | **9.2x** | **3008 KB** | **91.4%** |

**Proposed §1 replacement** for the "not for want of aiming" clause:

> a bitmask sheds none of it, and not for want of aiming: confining the stream
> from eight of sixteen ways to **one** leaves a 9.2$\times$ residual while
> **restoring 91\% of the neighbour's L3 occupancy** --- the mask recovers the
> capacity and the harm is not capacity.

### D2. The harm is L3-domain-local (`BERGAMO_BACKINVAL_OUTCOME`, confirmed twice)

Same-CCX 27.8x vs **other-CCX 1.30x/1.31x at matched bandwidth (24.8 GB/s)**.
Rules out fabric/CXL-link bandwidth. Belongs beside `tab:amdcat`.

### D3. It is **L3 eviction**, not back-invalidation (`AMD_L3OCC_OUTCOME`)

Victim L3 occupancy falls to **5.5%** of quiescent. This matters because it
**removes the last candidate for a reachable H3 charge** and is the third leg of
the H3 decision. Appendix, near `tab:h3sf`.

### D4. `prefetchnta` does not avoid allocation (`AMD_NARROWMASK_OUTCOME`)

On Zen 4c the victim is pinned at **100.00%** L2 miss under `wb_load`,
`wb_prefetchnta` **and** `wb_ntdqa` alike, all at ~25 GB/s. This closes the most
obvious reviewer objection --- *"use a non-temporal hint"* --- with a measurement
on current silicon. It belongs in the **body**, in the four-way comparison.
